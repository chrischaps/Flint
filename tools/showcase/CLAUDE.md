# tools/showcase: the showcase film rig (moved out of the root CLAUDE.md by #755)

This is the second half of the root file's "Offline capture" bullet; the first half (`--frame-step`, `--input-script`, `--pixels-per-point`, `--look`) is in `crates/flint-player/CLAUDE.md`.

- The showcase film's rig: `python tools/showcase/capture.py [beats] [--verify] [--scale 0.5]` captures each beat of `tools/showcase/film.toml` (scenes built from `examples/film/set.toml` + `examples/film/beats/*.toml` by `build_scenes.py`) to PNG sequences outside the repo; storyboard `docs/design/showcase-film.md`, book guides/filming-a-showcase.md. Its music (#438): `python tools/showcase/music.py [cut] [--plot] [--verify]` arranges `score.py` to a cut list (`tools/showcase/cuts/<cut>.toml`, shots in bars at 100 bpm, a bar = 2.4 s = 72 frames at 30 fps), synthesizes six stems (`synth.py`, no samples), renders the generated `music/<cut>.suite.toml` + `.events.toml` with `flint render-suite` and masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP as `~/flint-film/music/many-hands-<cut>.wav`, exactly the cut's length. The same list drives the picture, one file and one editor per cut (#437, #593): `tools/showcase/cuts/<name>.toml` (`master` 16:9 4K60, `reel` 9:16 30 fps; shots in bars, `tail_bars` where the last take runs on, `titles = "lower_third"|"caption"`, `points_wide`, `[encode]`, `[web]`): `capture.py --cut <name>` captures what it uses at its size and rate (`--scale` keeps the point canvas), `python tools/showcase/cut.py <name>` composes the titles in a worker pool and encodes with the WAV (master MP4, web MP4/WebM + poster; the reel and its story pieces), refusing a track whose `many-hands-<cut>.json` was scored to another list, and `check_cut.py <name>` checks every file's frames, its sound (length, -14 LUFS, true peak) and that every shot starts on the music's frame. The film's camera moves (`film::orbit`/`dolly`) reframe themselves in a portrait window (`film::portrait()`)
