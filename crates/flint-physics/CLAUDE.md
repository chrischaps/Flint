# flint-physics: joints (moved out of the root CLAUDE.md by #755)

The Rapier version gotcha and the fixed timestep are at the end of this file (from the root, #992 and #1337). The character controller's split between the frame (look, jump press) and the fixed step (walk, fall), and its starting facing (`start_yaw`): `crates/flint-player/CLAUDE.md`.

- **Physics joints** (ADR 0069) -- `joint` component (hinge/prismatic/spherical/fixed) on a dynamic entity, attached to `joint.parent` or the transform parent. Bodies are placed from `get_world_matrix` (rigid: scale is the collider's, next bullet) and dynamic poses write back as local `position` + `rotation_quat`, divided by the parent's world scale; a joint's `anchor` stays metres along the child's axes, unscaled (scenes are written that way: motion_hold's bobs), and a scaled jointed body is warned once (`joint_scale_warned`). TOML units are degrees/metres, converted to Rapier radians at sync. Scripts drive `motor_target` via `set_joint_target` and pose rest-quaternion nodes with `rotate_local` / `set_rotation_quat` (`set_rotation` still deletes the quaternion). Bench: `flint-examples/joint_test.scene.toml`
- **Scale, layers, mesh shapes, the reverse map** (#729; `sync.rs` `collider_shape`, `mesh_shape`, `groups_of`; `layers.rs`; tests `layer_tests.rs`) -- **Scale**: `world_scale` (the world matrix's axis lengths, `unit_snapped` so an unscaled rotated entity is exactly 1 and keeps its old bits) multiplies `size`, `offset` and the bounds centre; exact for any shape when uniform, else for an unrotated box, a capsule/cylinder with x == z, and mesh/convex; otherwise one `tracing::warn!` per entity (`shape_warned`) naming what it took. Shapes are made with the body and remade when the scale moves (next bullet). **Layers**: `CollisionLayers` (`default` is bit 0, then the project's `[project.physics] layers`, `Project::physics_layers` in flint-core, at most 31 more), set by the host with `set_layers` before `initialize` and kept by `clear`. Every collider gets `collision_groups(groups_of(col))`: membership = `collider.layer` (an unknown one is `bad-enum-value` and reads `default`), filter = `collider.mask` (every layer when unsaid; an unknown entry is reported as `mask[i]` and left out). Both unknowns are told by `flint_core::collision_layers::unknown_layer` (#1220), which `flint_scene::diagnose` raises too (`unknown_layers`, against `Project::physics_layers`), so `flint check` finds them before the game runs in the same words; keep the two on the one helper. `CollisionLayers::unknown`'s hint (scripts' mask names) is its `unknown_layer_hint`. No layer and no mask is `(GROUP_1, ALL)`, which meets everything as `all()/all()` did. Host trimeshes (terrain, splines) go in on `default` and take their entity's `collider` layer at the next `sync_to_rapier` (`trimesh_pending`). Queries: `LayerMask::query_groups` is `(ALL, mask)`, so a ray meets colliders on its mask whatever their own masks; `raycast_masked`, `move_character_masked` (default: the collider's own groups, which the character controller uses too). **Mesh/convex**: physics reads no files; `PhysicsSystem::collider_mesh_requests(world)` lists `(entity, collider.model or model.asset)`, the host hands `Arc<ColliderMesh>` over with `set_collider_mesh` before the bodies are made, and `mesh_shape` bakes `collider.rotation`, `offset` and the scale into the points, cached in `mesh_shapes` by (Arc address, convex, bits). A dynamic body's `mesh` is made convex (Rapier has no trimesh-trimesh contacts), with a `needs-component` warning; no triangles, or none that make a shape, is a box of `size` with a warning. The player's side is `player_app/collider_meshes.rs` (requests read before `stage_scene`, since model expansion removes the parent's `model`; `flint_import::import_gltf_geometry` skips the textures; `ImportResult::triangles()` places the nodes and keeps the nearest LOD). **Triangles that come late** (#1222): `set_collider_mesh` after the body exists queues the entity in `reshape_pending`, and the next `sync_to_rapier` makes its collider again on the same body (`reshape`, through `make_collider`, the one collider builder); the same `Arc` again is a no-op. `mesh_shapes` holds each shape's `Arc<ColliderMesh>` beside it, so a freed address can't be reused by other triangles while cached, and a replaced mesh drops the entries only the cache still holds. A `runtime = true` model with no `collider.model` (`waits_for_runtime_mesh`), or an entity a host named with `expect_collider_mesh` (a procgen model still queued), is a box of `size` with only a debug line. The player's side (`collider_meshes.rs`): `ColliderSources` on `GameCore` keeps procgen meshes' triangles by name (the resolver's uploads go through its `Recording` sink, at load, for late models and for the queue's completions) and runtime meshes' from `SceneRenderer::ray_mesh` (a `ray_target` upload; `RayMesh::positions` / `triangles`); `refresh` runs before the fixed steps each frame and walks the colliders only when the made generation, `SceneRenderer::runtime_mesh_generation` or `world.next_id()` moved. Tests: `layer_tests.rs` (`a_runtime_models_collider_waits_quietly_*`, `a_mesh_handed_over_again_*`, `a_ball_rests_on_a_convex_hull_handed_over_after_its_body`), the player's `made_collider_mesh_tests.rs` (GPU). **Reverse map**: `collider_entity` beside `collider_map`, kept by `insert_collider` / `remove_collider`; contacts, rays and `overlap_rect` use `entity_of`, never a scan.
- **Changes after the body exists** (#1221; `sync.rs` `update_colliders`, `ColliderSeen`, `rescale_collider`; tests `change_tests.rs`, cost `collider_pass_cost` `--ignored`) -- each step, after the kinematic poses, `update_colliders` brings made colliders up to the ECS: `is_sensor`, `layer`, `mask` and the world scale. It walks `seen` (one `ColliderSeen` a collider, made by `insert_collider`, holding its handle, and filled by `watch` wherever `make_collider` runs, #1222's `reshape` included), and a collider whose entity's `FlintWorld::write_stamp` and its ancestors' (`ScaleSeen::chain`, nearest first) haven't moved is skipped unread: a parent link that changes moves the child's stamp, so the chain's ids hold while the stamps do. `sync_from_rapier` absorbs its own pose write (the stamp just before it equals `seen.stamp`), so a falling body isn't looked at; the character controller's write isn't absorbed (one look a step). A look compares the raw `layer`/`mask` values and resolves `groups_in` only when they differ (waking the body when the groups change), and `refresh_chain` compares each `transform.scale` as written; only a changed one asks `world_scale`, and only one past `close` (a part in a thousand) of `ScaleSeen::built` remakes the shape through `collider_shape` (so `mesh_shapes`), with `set_position_wrt_parent`, a wake and `touch_static` on a fixed body. A rotation under a non-uniformly scaled parent that changes the world scale isn't heard (only `scale` writes are). `PhysicsSync::rereads` counts looks, resolves, scale asks and remade shapes for the tests. Measured (release, 2000 static colliders, a quarter parented, nothing written): 26 us a step, the old sensor-only pass 81 us. Scripts: `set_collision_layer` / `set_collision_mask` (`flint-script/src/api/physics.rs`), which check the names at the call
- **The terrain's collider is a heightfield** (#1219; `sync.rs` `register_static_heightfield`, `lib.rs` `StaticHeightfield`, tests `heightfield_tests.rs`) -- the player hands over `flint_terrain::Terrain::height_grid()` (the chunks' shared vertex grid, `chunks * chunk_resolution + 1` a side, each height the trimesh's) with the entity's offset as `origin`; physics builds Parry's `HeightField` with every cell `ZIGZAG_SUBDIVISION` (the chunk mesh's diagonal, (x0, z0) to (x1, z1); Parry's default is the other one: with it the comparison tests fail, a ray meeting another face inside a cell and the walking character standing 1.1 cm higher) and `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES`, translated by half its size since Parry centres it. Like a host trimesh it starts on `default` and takes its entity's `collider` layer at the next sync (`trimesh_pending`). `ColliderExtents::Heightfield` (script `shape: "heightfield"`), its centre placed by the collider's offset. Measured against the chunks' trimesh (`heightfield_tests`): 928 rays within 1.1e-5 m and the same face normals; a character walking 23 m across two seams within 1.4 mm, `grounded` differing on one step in 330; a ball rolling down a 1-in-4 slope never leaves it, where the trimesh threw it 0.19 m up at a cell edge (x = 42). `register_static_trimesh` stays for splines. Not done: `shape = "heightfield"` on a `collider` reading a heightmap file
- **Components are read through the typed view** (ADR 0115 step 4, #685; the view itself: `crates/flint-core/CLAUDE.md`) -- `rigidbody`, `collider`, `joint`, `character_controller` via `world.view(id, comp::X)`; the allowed strings are `sync::BODY_TYPES` (`static`, `dynamic`, `kinematic`, `kinematic_position`, `kinematic_velocity`), `COLLIDER_SHAPES`, `JOINT_TYPES` (with the `slider` / `ball` aliases). An unknown one is reported once and takes the default it always took (static, box, hinge). **The defaults in these readers are the real ones**: the schema files for these components write fields under `[component.x]` with no `.fields`, so they load with no fields and their defaults are never applied; where the two differ (`rigidbody.angular_damping` reader 0.0 / schema 0.05; eye height with no `character_controller.height` reader 1.5 / schema 1.8 * 0.85 = 1.53; the schema's `body_type` values lack the two `kinematic_*` spellings) the reader's stands until someone decides. `bounds` (`compute_bounds_center`) and `sprite` (`sprite_anchor_offset_y(world, id)`) go through the view too since #802. **A collider with no rigidbody** is never given to Rapier (`sync_to_rapier` loops over `rigidbody`); `report_lone_colliders` says so (`needs-component`) the second pass it sees one, so a script adding the two a step apart is not told off. Terrain and spline trimeshes are in `synced_entities` and are skipped. `flint_scene::diagnose` says it before the game runs too (`lone_collider`, #804: a `collider` written or from the archetype, no `rigidbody`, no `terrain` or `spline_mesh`), so `flint validate` and the load summary carry it; keep the two messages the same. Tests: `lib.rs` (`an_unknown_body_type_warns...`, `a_collider_without_a_rigidbody_warns_once`, `an_integer_move_speed_moves_at_that_speed`, which walks a character for a second)
- **Navigation** (ADR 0130, #969; `nav.rs`, tests `nav_tests.rs`, cost `nav_cost` `--ignored`) -- `NavGrid::new(NavSettings)` (`validate` first: `cell` under the agent's diameter) answers `find_path(&physics, from, to) -> Option<Vec<[f32; 3]>>` and `nearest_floor`. Columns `cell` square from the world origin, each a list of floor heights (`Tile`: 16 x 16, `heights` + `start` offsets), baked when a search first reaches the tile by `bake_column`: a ray down from over the fixed colliders' bounds, `solid = false`, on from 0.01 under each hit (a ray from inside a solid meets its underside, which faces down); a hit is a floor when its normal is within `max_slope_deg` and `has_room` (a cylinder of the radius from `lift` up, and a thin core from just over the floor, which catches a floor met from inside a solid and a low beam). Queries go through the grid's **own** `QueryPipeline` (`refresh` rebuilds it from the collider set), so a grid works before the first step, and through `QueryFilter::only_fixed().exclude_sensors()`: doors, lifts and agents aren't on it. **`PhysicsWorld::static_generation`** (process-wide counter, bumped by `insert_collider_with_parent` / `remove_rigid_body` / `remove_collider` on a fixed body and by `update_colliders` turning a fixed collider sensor or remaking its shape) is what `refresh` compares; never a count. A\* (`search`) over `Node { ix, iz, level }`, ties broken by node so a path is the same every run, bounded by `max_nodes`; `pull_straight` two passes over `walkable_line`, which also holds a leg's height within `FOLLOW` (0.1 m) of the floor, so a path bends at a ramp's foot and top. A test's own `sys.raycast` sees nothing until a `fixed_update` has updated Rapier's pipeline: `nav_tests::ready` steps once. The script side is `flint-script/src/api/nav.rs`
