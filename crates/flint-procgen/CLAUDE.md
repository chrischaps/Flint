# flint-procgen: living props, their knobs, the wooze field, Woozy Grove (moved out of the root CLAUDE.md by #755)

The wooze field's code is in `flint-wooze` (its knobs, presets and clocks over flint-core's general `step_clock` and `field::ParamField`, #747, ADR 0123), this crate's `scene_tables` (`WoozeDef`, the scene's `[wooze]` and `[living]`; moved from flint-scene by #686) and the player; its note is here beside its main consumer. Shallow wooze and shading boil: `crates/flint-render/CLAUDE.md`. Living Props A/B: `crates/flint-player/CLAUDE.md`. `flint gen` flags and `flint render --time`: `crates/flint-cli/CLAUDE.md`.

## Living props

- **Living props** (epic #206) -- a `LivingGenerator` splits a prop into `structure(spec, lineage_seed)` (segments with stable `id`/`parent`, leaf cards bound to segments; a tree's is exactly what `flint gen --seed <lineage>` bakes) and `pose(spec, &skeleton, shape_seed)` (per-segment rotation, length and radius scale, relative to the structure). `tree_v1` regrows its poses by default (`[living] pose = "regrow"`), which only the built-in L-system can; space colonization and text grammars report themselves unsupported unless `[living] pose = "noise"` (#506, `generators/tree/noise_pose.rs`; `PoseSource`, `NoiseConfig` = `[living.noise] turn/wavelength/stretch`, both left out of a spec's text at their defaults so its hash holds): a pose keeps the lineage's segments and bends each by seeded plane-wave noise at its midpoint, no twist (the prototype's field from `living_spike noise`, which now drives this). A colonization skeleton's `depth` is rewritten to branch order (`set_branch_orders`: the thickest, then straightest child carries its parent on) after leaves are placed, so per-level stagger and the variance weight read 3 levels on the pine, not 31 growth steps; a grammar's bracket depth is left alone. `assign_bones` folds the terminal whose folded run would be shortest (own length + longest run already folded into it): tips first, so trees folding a few tips are unchanged, and a colonized pine loses a step per twig in turn (folded ends 1.5 cm off on average, 9.4 cm at worst; `the_fold_keeps_a_folded_twig_within_a_few_centimetres`). `form_v1` refuses `pose = "noise"`. Example: `flint-examples/noise_trees.scene.toml` with `flint-examples/specs/grove_{pine,willow}`. `form_v1` (`generators/form/`, for Embodiment) is the second: one abstract form swept around a spine of bones, with two-sided cards that boil (scattered, or `card_layout = "rows"` as lines of writing; `body_opacity` below 1 draws the body translucent, 0 not at all, #575; `[living] softness` 0..1 feathers the body's outline at run time, #582: alpha by |N.V| in `skinned_shader.wgsl`'s `edge_feather`, riding `MaterialUniforms::unlit.z` via `set_entity_part_softness` on `LivingRig::body_parts`; a soft-only body draws through `soft_edge_pipeline`, depth written, and still casts, `SkinnedDrawCall::soft_edge_only`), and poses drawn from a seeded curve (`bend_deg`, `ripple`, `swell`, `turn_deg`, `stretch`, and `slide` along the section's broad axis, the one term that doesn't pivot at the base: `SegmentPose::slide`, absolute and not carried to children, zero for trees; full size at `drift.amplitude = 1`) rather than regrown. `pose` is `full_pose` held to `[living] variance` (#229): each segment pulled toward the skeleton by `amount × ((1 − steady) + steady × (depth / max_depth)^1.3)`, then with `keep_size` one shared length scale centres the box's sides on the rest pose's; `LivingSkeleton::with_variance` re-holds a `full_pose` without regrowing, and `VarianceConfig::FULL` is bit-identical to `full_pose`. `[living]` in a `*.procgen.toml` is validated at parse. `flint gen` on a supported spec bakes its skinned rest from `lineage_seed` (#209): bone 0 is the trunk, then one bone per segment, and `LivingGenerator::rig` returns the mesh with `LivingRig::palette`, which turns a pose or a blend into bone matrices. Reach it from a `dyn Generator` with `as_living()`. Book: concepts/procgen.md
- **Living props** (epic #206) -- a `living_prop` component on a model whose asset names a `[living]` spec. `flint_living::LivingProps` (the host's, beside its `ProcGenResolver`, #1024) keeps one skeleton and rig per spec and lineage, and per prop a ring of poses (plus one spare) a few steps ahead of its clock, filled by `LivingProps::process` after the resolver's `process_frame` with whatever's left of the frame budget; poses have their own cache, since the resolver's queue dedupes by spec name. The player rewrites `model.asset` to `<spec>@<lineage>` and sets `runtime`, uploads through `load_skinned_model`, and gives each prop its own bone buffer. Shape seeds come from the entity's *name* plus the step index. `--exit-after` prints `[living]` lines on whether rings stayed full and within budget, and what blending cost. Each frame (#212) `LivingProps::blend_prop` reads every part's place on the warped clock, blends its two poses through `LivingPose::blend_from` (which the strip shares) and writes only what moved through `write_entity_bones`; 40 oaks cost about 0.35 ms. A scene's `[living]` table holds `shadows = "blended" | "drift"` and `echoes`. Leaf jitter fits in the palette: the last `BOIL_BONES` bones hold only an offset, and a card's vertices weight them by `n x r` (`CardBoil`, `LivingRig::runtime_joints`), which glTF forbids, so those weights go only to the renderer and never into the baked GLB. Leaf tints are quantized and set per part with `set_entity_part_tint`, so a grove shares a few materials. Echoes (#228): `[living] echoes = { count = 6, spread = 0.9, alpha = 0.2 }` in the spec, or field by field in a scene's `[living]`; `count = 0` is pixel-identical to none and costs nothing. Each echo is `LivingProps::blend_echo` (the prop's own blend at an offset time) into `write_entity_echo_bones`, which is what makes it exist; a spec's `[living] echo_blend = "lighten"` draws them with a max blend of premultiplied colour (`set_entity_echo_lighten`, `fs_skinned_lighten`), so they can only add light, and `[living] echo_slide = <steps>` gives the poses' slide to the echoes alone (the prop never slides; an echo's grows with its `|offset|`, whole at that many steps; `LivingConfig::slide_shown`, and `DrawnBones::slide` so a hold can't hide a change), and `[living] echo_places = { at = [[along, up, steps], ...], from, whole }` gathers the copies onto a few set places once the spread passes `from` (bit for bit the continuous spread at or below it; one copy a place from `whole`, `EchoConfig::copies_placed`; offsets along `LivingSkeleton::place_axes`, `form_v1`'s its lines and its length, a tree's X and Y; a placed copy's slide fades, `EchoCopy::slide_shown`; `DrawnBones::place`; #610); the renderer draws it front faces only with no depth write, after everything opaque, reusing the entity's transform and sharing materials by content. 40 oaks with 6 each add about 3.7 ms a frame, mostly palettes. With echoes on, `flint gen --strip` draws through the same skinned path. Bench: `flint-examples/living_props.scene.toml`. Book: concepts/procgen.md
- **Living Props knobs** (#218) -- the F3 **Living Props** panel (`flint-debug-ui/src/living_props_panel/`, in any scene with a `living_prop`) and Rhai's `tune_living(map)`, `freeze_living(bool)`, `step_living()`, `save_living_preset(name)`, `load_living_preset(name)` share one path, `player_app/living_tuning.rs`: a change is a `flint_procgen::WoozePreset` whose unset fields leave a knob alone, so a preset file loads exactly what a person turned. A ring keeps each pose twice (`RingPose`: `full` as it grew, `held` at the lineage's variance) and the cache only the first, so `LivingProps::tune_variance` re-holds with no regrowing and reaches cached poses: poses in use across the grove first, then the rest taking turns with filling. While a knob moves, filling goes to the pose needed soonest (`pose_needed_soonest`), ahead of re-holds and of back-poses for waiting clocks; at rest rings fill as they always did, which keeps frames byte-identical. `tune_echoes` / `tune_stagger` reshape the ring to cover old and new clocks, and hold the new ones as `PendingClocks` until `ring.holds` what they read, so `frames_starved` counts only what's in use (`in_use_start`, not `ring.first`). Hidden echoes keep their buffers (`set_entity_echo_count`, opacity 0). A preset file is `wooze/<name>.wooze.toml` beside the scene or one level up; a host fills it into the scene's tables after `load_scene` (`flint_procgen::settle_scene`, which runs `apply_preset_file`; `PlayerApp::apply_scene_settings` does it too), so everything downstream only sees knobs, and the scene's own values win except for `[boil]` and stagger over a component's. Freeze is `living_dt` 0; a step is `LivingProps::advance_beats` before the tick, a whole beat per prop whatever its step. `flint-examples/living_sweep.scene.toml` moves every knob: its `--exit-after` lines should say 0 frames missing a pose and 0 kept their last bones. **`tune_living(map, seconds)`** (#514; `player_app/living_ramps.rs`): the grove's clock knobs go to `tune_field(.., seconds)` (the field's ramps; an ease compares with `WoozeField::global_target`, so asking every frame still arrives), the other continuous knobs (`Eased`: echo spread/alpha, variance amount/steady, softness, the jitter rate, and a species' clock) become `KnobRamps` on `flint_core::field::Ramp` (public for this), stepped at the top of `LivingSubsystem::tick` on `living_dt` and given to the props through the free `tune_props` / `tune_species`; the discrete rest goes through the stepped path at once. Every stepped method (`tune_living_as`, `tune_species`, `replace_species`, `clear_living_species`, `reset_living_knobs`) cancels the ramps on what it sets. Tests: `living_ramps_tests.rs`. Book: guides/debug-panels.md, concepts/procgen.md, concepts/scripting.md (Living Props API)

- **The jitter clock** (#529) -- the boil (leaf-card turns, shade tints; `LivingJitter`) is a pure function of seconds, read at `LivingProp::jitter_now()` = its own `jitter_time` + `jitter_ahead`. `jitter_time` starts at the props' clock and moves on by `dt * rate` in `tick` and `advance_beats`, the rate from `jitter_rate_layers` (base 1, the scene's `[living] jitter_rate`, a species' own; `tune_jitter_rate`, clamped to `0..=JITTER_RATE_MAX`). So a new rate changes how fast it runs, never where it is: nothing pops, each channel holds until its next tick. Times 1.0 is exact, so an untuned prop's `jitter_time` is `LivingProps::time()` to the bit (`an_untuned_boil_reads_the_props_clock_to_the_bit`): never read the jitter at `time()` directly. An echo reads `jitter_now() + offset * jitter_step`, the same clock. Example: `flint-examples/living_boil_rate.scene.toml`. Book: concepts/procgen.md, "Leaf jitter"
- **Drifting rows** (#576) -- `form_v1`'s `row_pace` (m/s, 0 = none) and `row_pace_spread` give `LivingRig::rows: Option<RowDrift>` (`living.rs`; built by `cards::row_drift` from the `RowLayout` that `rows()` now returns beside the mesh, on its own rng fork `"row pace"`, so `row_pace = 0` is byte-identical: no rows, no bones, `palette_len()` = segments + `BOIL_BONES`). The runtime palette is `[segments][BOIL_BONES][a bone per RowWord]`: a word (or neighbours on one line when words outnumber `MAX_SKINNED_BONES - first`) rides its own bone = its line's segment bone x `word_move` (rest frame on the line's sampled `RowLine` track to the frame where it shows, squashed along the line to the part inside the margins, `RowDrift::shown`, which for a plain stroke reads as clipped; the word's boil turn folded in, so its cards' `CardBoil` weights are zero and `runtime_joints` gives them `[word bone]` alone). Every palette site calls `rig.fast_bones_into(turns, row_time, out)` / `still_bones_into` (rest palette, shadow, `gen --strip`) instead of `boil_bones`, so a tree is unchanged. Clock: `LivingProp::row_time` (starts at 0, `+= dt * row_rate`, in `tick` and `advance_beats`), echoes read `echo_row_time` (offset x the add-time step). `blend_bones` keeps `DrawnBones::segments` for rigs with rows and re-uploads from bone 0 when only the words or turns moved. Knob `row_rate` is #529's `jitter_rate` mirrored through every layer (`tune_row_rate`, `ROW_RATE_MAX` 8, scene/species/preset tables, schema, F3 `RowRate`, `Eased::RowRate`, `--report json`'s `row_rate` plus the prop's `row_time`). Tests: `generators/form/tests.rs` (drifting), `living_props/tests.rs` (`a_new_row_rate_*`, `an_echo_shows_*`), the player's `the_row_rate_steps_eases_*`. Example: `flint-examples/drifting_page.scene.toml` (+ `scripts/drifting_page.rhai`). Book: concepts/procgen.md, Drifting rows
- **Where the living props' code lives** (#753) -- the runtime is the **`flint-living` crate** (#1024; it was this crate's `src/living_props/`): `lib.rs` (the request, stats, lineages, `LivingProps` and adding a prop), `layers.rs` (settings resolution: `LivingDefaults`, the species, clock, echo and variance overrides, `LivingLayer`/`Resolved`, and every `tune_*`), `ring.rs` (`PoseRing`, `PoseCache`), `prop.rs` (`LivingProp`: clocks, echoes, what they ask of the ring), `schedule.rs` (the budgeted fill on `web_time::Instant`, re-holds, building lineages), `blend.rs` (`BlendFrame`, `blend_prop`/`blend_echo`), `ab.rs` (`fork`, `swap_cache`, `redraw_all`), `seam.rs` (every entry point that was a resolver method), `tests.rs` (driven through `test_grove.rs`'s `Grove`, a resolver and props as a host keeps them). **Every knob resolves through `layers::Layer`**: `Option<T>` for a single value, and `field_layer!` makes `ClockOverride`/`EchoOverride`/`VarianceOverride` (`over`, `full`, `merged` from one field list); a prop keeps a `Layered<L>` per knob (`base`: the spec's or entity's, `global`: the scene's `[living]` and every knob moved live), its species an override, and `value(species)` is species over global over base. `tune_echoes`/`tune_stagger`/`tune_softness`/`tune_jitter_rate` are one `tune_props` call each, `tune_variance` the same over lineages; there is no `set_*` / `set_*_of` any more (a whole config is `tune_x(LivingScope::All, XOverride::full(c))`). `layers.rs`'s tests resolve a grove that is never built. The living layer names a structure's piece `living::Segment` (`BranchSegment`, which every living generator emits). **The seam** (#1024): the resolver holds nothing of the props'. It lends `living_spec(name)` (the spec checked to live, and the identity its poses are cached under), `registry()`, and `budget_left_ms()` (its queue's budget less what `process_frame` used); `LivingProps::add_prop(&mut resolver, request)`, `make_now`, `process(&resolver, dt, wooze)` (after `process_frame`), `step_now` (no budget), `process_beside`, `settle`. The player keeps them in `GameCore::living_props`. flint-living depends on this crate for `LivingGenerator`, `living.rs`'s rig and poses, the spec types and the registry; `JITTER_RATE_MAX` / `ROW_RATE_MAX` stay here (`living.rs`), since a preset file's rates are held to them, and flint-living re-exports them. This crate still links flint-wooze for `scene_tables` and `living.rs` (#1330). **Every knob is one row** (#753, #1025): `living_knobs.rs`' `living_knob_rows!` (a `#[macro_export]` callback macro: `living_knob_rows!(my_macro)` hands `my_macro!` every row) holds each knob's variant, field, type, default, key, `WoozePreset` path (`wooze.x`, `living.x`, `echoes.x`, `variance.x`) and the panel's section, control and help (those three are flint-debug-ui's tokens, resolved where the macro is called, so this crate never reads them). This crate makes `LivingKnob` from it (`ALL`, `COUNT`, `key`, `index`, `is_clock`, `default_value`, and each knob's slot in a preset, a `[living]`, a species table, `WoozeKnobs` and a `WoozeSample`: `in_preset` / `set_in_preset` / `clear_in_preset`, `in_living`, `take_from_living`, `in_species`, `take_from_species`, `in_wooze`, `in_sample`), `KnobValue` (`Number`, `Count`, `Flag`, `Name`; compares with an `f32`, `u32`, `bool` or `Stagger` for tests), `species_keys()` (what `clear_living` takes), `species_def_of` and `wooze_knobs_of`. flint-debug-ui's `Knob` is `LivingKnob` re-exported, its `knob_table!` makes `KNOB_TABLE`, `LivingKnobs` and `KnobFlags` from the same rows (`KnobPanel` gives `desc()` / `in_section`), and flint-living resolves each knob (`ResolvedProp::get(knob)`, `SpeciesOverride::get` / `clear` / `clear_keys` / `to_def`, `LivingProps::global_def`, and `defs.rs`: the `[living]` and species tables into layers, `tune_global`, `apply_preset`, moved from the player), each an exhaustive `match`, so the compiler names every place a new row needs the runtime. flint-player names no knob: `LivingView` is a `KnobRead` per `LivingKnob`, the ramps ease every knob whose value is a number, and `flint render --report json`'s `values` is a loop. Adding a knob: a row in `living_knob_rows!`, then the runtime's resolution (its layer, `resolved`, `SpeciesOverride`'s three matches, `defs.rs`' checks). The rest of the panel: `mod.rs` (the panel and its selection), `state.rs` (the host's DTOs and actions), `ui.rs` (sections, controls, marks), `overlay.rs` (labels, the picked tree's window, `pick_prop` and the projection), `clock.rs` (the warped-clock drawing), `tests.rs`. `widgets::section` and `widgets::mixed_slider` are every panel's

## Sweeps

- **`sweep_v1`** (#750, `generators/sweep.rs`) -- a profile swept along an inline path, through `flint_geometry::sweep` (the sweep a scene's `spline_mesh` draws, so the two make the same triangles). Params: `[[params.control_points]]` as a `.spline.toml`'s (`position`, `twist`, `gap_after`), `closed` (default false, unlike a spline file's true), `spacing` (0.5, at least 0.01), `profile` / `width` + `height`, `profile_closed`, `smooth`, `offset`, `texture_length`, `color` (hex), `metallic`, `roughness`. The path is inline on purpose: the cache keys on the spec's bytes, so a spline file the spec named could change under a cached mesh. No stripes (a `MeshData` has no vertex colour) and no collider (the resolver makes none); those are `spline_mesh`'s. Over 4M estimated vertices is `BudgetExceeded`. Tangents come from `compute_tangents`. Example: `specs/examples/brass_coil.procgen.toml`. A plain `flint render` doesn't run the resolver, so a scene naming a `sweep_v1` spec draws it in the player only (bake it with `flint gen`). `param_ui::type_cell` names a list of numbers and a list of number lists now (it said "list of strings")

## Texture ops

- **One descriptor per op** (#720; `generators/texture/descriptor.rs`) -- each `[[params.ops]]` op is an `OpDescriptor` beside its struct in its family's module (`pub const BRICK_GRID: OpDescriptor`): type name, label, summary, the channels it reads/writes/modifies, and every key as a `ParamDesc` (`P::int/float/flag/channel/opt_channel/choice/color/tables`, `.min/.max/.range/.help`), plus `build`, which reads each key through `OpParams` (`p.u32("columns")?`, `p.choice(..)`, `p.tables("stops")?`). The registry is `pipeline::OPS`; `ALL_OP_TYPES` is computed from it in a const block. `op_param_schema`, `op_default_params`, `parse_op`, the ops' `port_info` (a provided method on `TextureOp`, which asks only `descriptor()`) and the README's op catalog (`op_reference_markdown`, `param_catalog -- ops`, held by `tests/examples.rs`) are all derived. Adding an op: struct, `impl TextureOp` with `fn descriptor`, the const, one line in `OPS`. **Where** (#1026): `ops/` is one module per family, `layout` (grids, `domain_warp`), `cells` (per-cell values and mortar), `patterns` (noise and pattern generators, `NoiseType`), `blend`, `math` (math, `map_range`, `color_ramp`), `adjust`, `filters` (neighbourhood ops, `derive_normal`); `ops/mod.rs` holds `TextureOp`/`OpPortInfo` and re-exports every op, so `ops::X` paths hold. `algorithms/noise/` is the gradient noises and `Fbm` in `mod.rs`, `cellular.rs` (Worley, the custom Voronoi), `musgrave.rs`; also re-exported. Reading a key the descriptor doesn't declare panics, and `every_op_reads_exactly_the_keys_it_declares` builds every op and checks it read every declared key. Unset keys take the default; a wrong type, an unknown choice or a number outside `min`/`max` is `InvalidParameter { name: "ops[i].key" }` (bounds compare the TOML's f64, not its f32 rounding). A colour default is linear floats, shown in the schema as its nearest 8-bit hex (`cell_color`'s `#735947`), since those floats are what specs that omit the key have always rendered with. The A/B for a change here: `flint gen --seed 7` on every `texture_v1` spec plus a spec of every op at its defaults, hashes compared with main's

## Wooze field

- **Living props' scene tables** (#686; `src/scene_tables/`) -- `[living]` and `[wooze]` are this crate's, not flint-scene's: `SceneFile` keeps them as raw TOML in `tables` like any table it has no field for, and `scene_tables/mod.rs` holds their types (`LivingDef`, `LivingSpeciesDef`, `LivingEchoesDef`, `LivingVarianceDef`, `WoozeDef`, and a preset file's `WoozePreset`, `WoozeKnobs`, `BoilDef`; all re-exported at the crate root, where `flint_scene::X` used to be), `wooze.rs` the preset files, `WoozeDef::field` / `check` and `WoozeIssue` (flint-scene's old `wooze.rs`, moved whole), `commit.rs` `look_edits` / `commit_look` / `CommitReport` / `edit_species` (the generic `SceneEdit` / `SceneScalar` and `SceneDocument::apply_edit` stayed in flint-scene, `edit.rs`). **Reading**: `LivingTables::of(&scene_file) -> Result<LivingTables { living, wooze }, String>` (a key a table doesn't know or a wrong type is `Err("[living]: unknown field ...")`), `LivingTables::or_warn` for a reader after a settle, `.put(&mut scene_file)` to write them back. **`settle_scene(&mut scene_file, path)`** is what `flint_scene::load_scene` used to do for these tables: refuse a table that doesn't fit (`FlintError::TomlParseError("<file>: [living]: ...")`, no line number now, since the table is checked after the file parsed) and fill in the preset `[wooze] preset` names (`apply_preset_file`, issues warned). **Every host that plays or draws a scene calls it after `load_scene`**: `flint play`, `flint-player`, the web entry, a scene transition, `flint render` (`load_settled`), the viewer and its reload, `flint bundle`'s scene check; `PlayerApp::apply_scene_settings` settles a copy itself, so a game binary that calls it keeps its preset. `flint query` / `flint entity` don't (they read entities only). **A host that forgets is told** (#807): `apply_preset_file` takes out every `preset` that isn't a built-in (filling in its file, or returning `preset 'x' isn't a file in wooze/ or one of ...`), so a non-built-in name that reaches `WoozeDef::field` means the scene was never settled, and its issue says `preset 'x' was never filled in ... this host didn't call it` (every host warns field issues: `flint_player::field_of`, the viewer). `LivingTables::problems(&scene_file)` lists each of the two tables that doesn't fit, for a check that reports both. Hence the new dependencies: flint-procgen -> flint-scene, flint-schema; flint-debug-ui and flint-viewer -> flint-procgen. `tests/scene_table_schemas.rs` holds `schemas/scene/{living,wooze}.toml` to the types

- **Wooze field** (#213) -- `flint_wooze::WoozeField::sample(position, time)` returns `{ amplitude, step, glide, jitter }`, the one answer to how woozy a place is. A scene's `[wooze]` table (global knobs, `preset`) and `[[wooze.zone]]` entries (`shape = "sphere" | "box"`, `center`, `radius` / `size`, `falloff`, any knob) become it through `flint_procgen::WoozeDef::field`; `flint validate` fails on a bad zone, naming it, through `WoozeDef::check`, and the player warns and goes without it. No table, no field, nothing samples. In a scene with a field it decides `step` and `glide` for every prop, whatever the spec says. Rhai: `set_wooze(map[, seconds])`, `set_wooze_zone(name, map[, seconds])`, `remove_wooze_zone(name[, seconds])`; ramps are what make the field a function of time. A consumer must not pop on a change: count the clock in beats advanced by `dt / step`, and read glide and amplitude through a `flint_wooze::Cycle` (`read(beats, glide, amplitude)`; it is `flint_core::step_clock::Cycle`), which holds what a part started its cycle with until the cycle ends. The field is `flint_core::field::ParamField<4>` over `flint_wooze::WOOZE_KEYS` (`WoozeField::params()`); a new field by place (fog, wind) is a `static FieldKeys<N>` and gets zones, ramps and their messages free (ADR 0123), and a scene can declare one with no Rust at all, `[field.<name>]` (#1014, `flint_core::field::SceneField`). `amplitude` is applied per part with `SegmentPose::pulled`; `jitter` scales card turns, and goes into tints before they're quantized so materials stay few. Presets: `flint_wooze::PRESETS`, `linklater` the look the woozy grove settled on (#219). Schema: `schemas/scene/wooze.toml`, a `[table.wooze]` (#686) whose `zone` field is a `[[wooze.zone]]` entry; `flint schema wooze` prints both. Bench: `flint-examples/wooze_field.scene.toml`. ## Woozy Grove

- **Woozy Grove** (#217) -- `flint-examples/woozy_grove.scene.toml`, the walkable place the look gets judged: 38 living trees from `flint-examples/specs/grove_{oak,cypress,birch,thorn}.procgen.toml` (L-system, no `[[lod]]`), a bench and lantern on one `shallow_wooze`, zones `hush` / `drowse` / `shiver` around a clearing that takes `preset = "linklater"` (#219: amplitude 0.5, step 0.5, glide 0.6, jitter 1, with the scene's `boil_on = 1`; a built-in holds only the field's knobs, so a scene with that preset and the default `boil_on = 2` boils at half the rate). `tools/living-strip/living_strip.py` makes the book's before/after strip and slit-scan from the scene: a slit-scan (one row of pixels stacked over time) shows a lurch, hold or glide in a still, which frame diffs and contact sheets don't. Judge a look's cost by the `[living]` lines, not fps: a grove that has run out of poses still reports 60 fps, each starved prop simply keeping its last bones. Check them on a long run and under CPU load too; a 30 s idle run once showed 0 where a loaded one showed 3,016 of 3,560 frames short, because `fill_within` refused every pose while its estimate sat just under the budget. `flint-examples/woozy_grove_walk.txt` is a route through them for `--input-script`. `crates/flint-cli/tests/woozy_grove_cli.rs` keeps it loading.
