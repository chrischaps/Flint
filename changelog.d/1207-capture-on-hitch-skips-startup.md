### `--capture-on-hitch` passes over startup hitches; `--capture-on-hitch-startup` takes them (#1207)
**What changed.** `--capture-on-hitch[=N]` (on `flint-player` and `flint play`) now counts only hitches at or after t = 2 s of game time, the same cutoff the exit report's `startup hitches` line, the hitch log's `startup` flag and `--max-hitches` use. Before, a game's first N hitches were nearly always its loading (script warm-up, physics catch-up, first pipeline compiles), so the captures held the load and not the hitch felt mid-game. The first startup hitch it passes over is said once on the console (`[capture] passed over a startup hitch at t=1.32 s ...`). The new `--capture-on-hitch-startup` (which needs `--capture-on-hitch`) takes those too. A game binary calls `PlayerApp::capture_startup_hitches(true)` after `capture_frames`.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless you capture the load on purpose: then add `--capture-on-hitch-startup`.
