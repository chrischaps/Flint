### Mouse look turns the same at any frame rate; `character_controller` gains `start_yaw` and six tuning fields (#728)
**What changed.** The character controller took mouse look and the jump press inside each fixed physics step. A 30 fps frame (two steps) turned twice as far, and a frame with no step (about half of all frames at 144 Hz) lost its motion and its jump. Now the player takes both once a frame (`PhysicsSystem::character_frame_input`, before the fixed loop), and `update_character` reads only held keys. New `character_controller` fields, defaults as before: `start_yaw` (degrees about +Y; 0 faces +Z, 90 faces +X), `look_sensitivity` (0.172 degrees per raw mouse unit, which was 0.003 rad), `pitch_limit` (80 degrees, was 1.4 rad), `sprint_multiplier` (1.6), `gravity_scale` (1, times the physics world's gravity, which was a fixed 9.81), `step_height` (0.5) and `snap_to_ground` (0.2).
**What to do.** An `--input-script` route with `look`, tuned at a frame step coarser than 1/60, now turns less: double its `look` amounts at 1/30 (the showcase trailer went from 450 to 900). A game that calls `update_character` itself should also call `character_frame_input` once a frame. A scene laid out along +Z to suit the old fixed facing can use `start_yaw` instead.
