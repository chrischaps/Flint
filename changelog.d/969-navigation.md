### Scripts can find a way round walls: `find_path` and `move_along_path` (#969)
**What changed.** A new `nav` script module. `find_path(fx, fy, fz, tx, ty, tz[, opts])` returns the way between two points as `[#{x, y, z}, ...]` on the floor, round walls and up ramps and steps, or `[]` when there is none. It works over the floors the scene's static colliders, terrain and spline roads make, baked into a grid a tile at a time as searches reach it. The agent is 0.8 m across, 1.8 m tall and climbs 0.35 m steps unless an opts map says otherwise (`radius`, `height`, `step`, `max_slope`, `cell`, `max_nodes`). `move_along_path(id, path, speed)` walks an entity along a path for the frame and returns `#{x, y, z, dir_x, dir_z, next, remaining, arrived}`. Doors, lifts and other agents are not on the grid yet. A script that declared its own `find_path` or `move_along_path` with these arities now shadows the engine's.
**What to do.** Nothing. To use it, see the book's Physics and Runtime page, "Navigation", and `examples/nav_lamplighter.scene.toml`.
