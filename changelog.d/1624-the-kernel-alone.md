### `flint init --template 2d` leaves out physics, first-person, rig, particles and terrain, and CI holds one dependency rule (#1624)
**Package.** engine
**What changed.** A new 2d project's `flint.toml` sets five standard packages to `false` under `[packages]` (`physics`, `first-person`, `rig`, `particles`, `terrain`), each with a comment saying what deleting the line brings back. It keeps sound, animation, flipbooks, procgen and the F3 panels. A walk or blank project still lists nothing, and an existing project is not touched. The engine's crate graph is now held to one rule (ADR 0138 decision 6), by `scripts/package-deps-check.py` in `scripts/ci.sh`: a kernel crate depends on no package's crate, and a package's crate depends only on the kernel and on what its `[requires]` lists. CI's new `kernel 2d` job builds the 2d template with every standard package left out, and plays it.
**What to do.** Nothing. To give a 2d game physics, delete its `physics = false` and `first-person = false` lines and run `flint build`. A package of your own in the engine's `packages/` lists every package its crates use under `[requires]`.
