### Creases in the shared edges mark two pixels at a fold too, so pencil and ASCII crease lines are thinner (#952)
**What changed.** The shared edge pass (`edges.wgsl`, read by `pencil`, `ascii`, `contour`, `edges_view` and user styles with `inputs = ["edges"]`) rebuilds each surface normal from one neighbour on each axis. Since #743 it took the neighbour nearer in view distance, which finds the right side of a depth step but not of a fold, where the distance runs on unbroken: the steeper face's last pixel took a tangent across the fold, and a crease marked a band 3 to 4 px wide (a pillar's foot seen square on, a box's vertical corner). Each tangent now comes from the side whose two neighbours lie on the pixel's own plane, so a fold marks the two pixels astride it. Crease lines in pencil, ASCII and `edges_view` are thinner at folds, and a few ASCII crease glyphs on small curved props change. Silhouettes are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game with its own pencil or ASCII goldens may see crease pixels move by a pixel and should re-bless them after a look.
