### `play_clip` and `stop_clip` work on a skinned character after its first frame (#732)
**What changed.** A skinned entity's `animator.clip` and `animator.playing` were read only when the engine first saw it, so `play_clip` never changed a tracked character's clip, `stop_clip` never paused it, and a finished one-shot never replayed. Both fields are now live, with the rules node animation already used: a new `clip` cuts to its start and cancels a crossfade in flight (`play_clip` also clears `blend_target`), `playing = false` holds the current frame, `true` resumes, and turning a finished one-shot back on replays it. The engine now writes the fields back so they say what plays: `clip` becomes the clip a crossfade lands on, `playing` turns `false` when a one-shot ends, and `true` when `autoplay` starts a clip.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a script calls `play_clip` every frame on a non-looping clip (it now replays each time it ends) or writes `clip` expecting no effect on a skinned entity (it now cuts). To tell a one-shot has finished, read `get_field(me, "animator", "playing")`.
