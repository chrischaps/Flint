### A glTF animation clip starts at its first key (#872)
**What changed.** Skeletal and node clips loaded from glTF now play from their earliest key. Blender writes frame N at N / fps, so a clip on frames 1 to 250 (every Mixamo clip through Blender) began at 1/30 s, and the engine held its first pose until then: frame 0 and frame 1 of a `--frame-step` capture or a `flint render --anim-time 0 --time` span drew the same pose while physics moved, and each loop paused for a frame on its seam. Every key now moves back by the clip's first one, so a clip's `duration` (and `AnimationSystem::get_clip_duration`, the previewer's scrubber, a non-looping clip's `on_animation_end`) is shorter by that lead-in, and a pose at time t is the one the file keys at t plus the lead-in. Clips already starting at 0 are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a clip relied on waiting before its first key: key a still pose at 0 to keep the wait.
