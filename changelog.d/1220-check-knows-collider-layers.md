### `flint check` and `flint validate` know the collision layers: an undeclared `collider.layer` or `mask` name is found before the game runs (#1220)
**What changed.** A `collider.layer`, or an entry of `collider.mask`, that is neither `default` nor one of the project's `[project.physics] layers` (written on the entity or given by its archetype) is now `bad-enum-value` in `flint check`, `flint validate`, `flint edit`'s panel and the scene's load summary, naming the nearest layer: `"playr" is not a collision layer`, hint ``did you mean `player`?; it is read as `default` ``. A project that declares no layers is told the line to add, `[project.physics] layers = ["player"]`. The player says the same words when the body is made, where it used to say `"playr" is not a value of `layer`` for a layer and repeat itself for a mask entry; what a bad name does is unchanged (a layer reads as `default`, a mask entry is left out).
**What to do.** Nothing, unless `flint check` now lists a layer: declare it under `[project.physics] layers` in `flint.toml`, or fix the spelling.
