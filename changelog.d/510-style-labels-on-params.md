### A Rust style's labels and help go on its params: `StyleHint` is gone (#510)
**What changed.** `StyleParam` and `StyleOption` take `.label(..)`, `.help(..)`, `.when(..)` and `.unless(..)` (and a param `.integer()`) themselves; `StyleDescriptor::hints` and `StyleHint` are removed. A colour's label and help go on its `_r` channel. Read them back with `StyleDescriptor::label_for`, `help_for`, `is_integer` or `ui(name)` (the old `hint(name)`). F4, `--list-styles` and Copy TOML show the same labels as before. In a `.shader.toml`, a `label` or `help` on a colour's `_r` param now names the colour's picker, where before it named the red channel alone.
**What to do.** A game with its own Rust style moves each `StyleHint::new(name, label, help)` onto the setting: `StyleParam::new("grain", 0.5, 0.0..=1.0).label("Paper grain").help("...")`, then drops `.hints(..)`. Scene files and `.shader.toml` files need nothing.
