### `flint edit <scene>` orbits like every other editor (#1153)
**What changed.** The scene viewer and the track editor (`--spline`) now use the orbit camera the model, procgen, terrain and particle editors use. A drag turns at the same rate (0.005 rad a pixel, half the viewer's old rate), right-drag pans by a share of the distance, and scroll zooms by 10% a notch instead of one unit. Dragging down now tilts the view to look from higher up, as in the other editors, and the view cube's drag turns the same way. A view cube axis click still eases onto that side, orthographic; a drag that orbits no longer picks the entity under the cursor; O and `[` `]` work in the track editor. In every editor an orbit tilts to 1.56 rad (the old previewers stopped at 1.4), and the turntable stops when a drag orbits, not at the first press. `--watch` is the editors' shared watch, with the same folders and the same rule for user styles' WGSL (#429), and a new WGSL file in a style folder no longer reloads the scene: on Windows the folder itself was reported changed right after the file, and that counted.
**What to do.** Nothing.
