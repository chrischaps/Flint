### `flint frame replay --draw-times` and `--overdraw`: what each draw costs, and how often a pixel is drawn (#1203)
**What changed.** A replay can now time each draw of the scene pass on the GPU, `--draw-times`, which puts a `gpu_ms` on every scene draw in the replay's `manifest.json` and lists the costliest draws and the cost by kind. `flint frame show --draws --sort gpu` and the frame debugger's draws table (a `GPU ms` column) sort by it. `--overdraw` adds an `Overdraw` pass after the scene's: `overdraw_count` counts the draws that shade each pixel, and `overdraw` shows that count as a heat map, each colour twice the draws of the one before (1, 3, 7, 15, 31+). Neither flag changes a pixel of the frame, and a frame drawn without them is drawn as before. Draw times need an adapter with timestamps inside passes (Vulkan, DX12, Metal); elsewhere the replay says so. A capture's `DrawDescription` gained an optional `gpu_ms`; older readers ignore it.
**What to do.** Nothing. To find a costly draw: `flint frame replay <capture> --draw-times --overdraw`, then `flint frame show <replay> --draws --sort gpu`.
