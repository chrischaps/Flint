### `flint frame open`: the frame debugger's window (#1088)
**What changed.** `flint frame open <capture>` opens a capture (from `flint render --frame-capture` or the player's `--capture-frame-at`, Shift+F2 and `--capture-on-hitch`) in a window. The passes are a tree with their GPU times and why a skipped one didn't run. The selected pass's image has zoom, pan, a texel readout of the stored values, channel, exposure and depth controls, and an A/B wipe against the pass before or the finished frame. Its draws are a sortable, filterable table that follows an entity through every pass. The CPU phases, GPU passes and the player's frame history run along the bottom. Up and Down step through the frame. `--pass`, `--target`, `--entity` and `--wipe` pick what it opens on, and `--screenshot out.png` draws one frame of the window with no window opened (`--json` answers `{"written": [...]}`). `flint frame image` now goes through the same texel mapping as the window, with the same output.
**What to do.** Nothing. Book: guides/frame-debugger.md.
