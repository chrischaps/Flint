### The `ascii` style's line glyphs hold still in motion (#477)
**What changed.** With `temporal = "on"` (the default) the edge glyphs (`edges = 1`) now keep a history of their own, as the tone glyphs have since #277. A cell keeps part of last frame's edge length, direction and place (the square root of `smoothing`), and keeps its decisions (a line or not, which line, which of two cells a line near their border belongs to, which corner) until the edge has clearly moved past them, by `hysteresis`. On the motion bench (`flint render examples/ascii_motion.scene.toml --frames 181 --orbit 6.8755 --flip-stats`) glyph reversals fell from 38.2 a frame to 0.9, and flips from 210 to 102. In a fast pan a marginal line waits until it clears the threshold band: about 6% fewer line cells at 1.2 rad/s. A single render, `temporal = "off"` and `hysteresis = 0` with `smoothing = 0` draw edges as before, byte for byte.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game that tuned `hysteresis` or `smoothing` for tone should know they now steady the edges too; `edges = 0` still turns line glyphs off.
