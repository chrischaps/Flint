### `LivingProps::tune_*` is the one way to turn a Living Props knob; the F3 panel's knobs say what they do (#753)
**What changed.** The runtime behind living props moved from one 5,000-line `living_props.rs` into `flint_procgen::living_props::{layers, ring, prop, schedule, blend, ab}`, and the F3 panel's into `flint-debug-ui`'s `living_props_panel/`, with every knob described once in a table. The whole-config setters `LivingProps::set_echoes`, `set_echoes_of`, `set_stagger`, `set_stagger_of`, `set_variance` and `set_variance_of` are gone: `tune_echoes`, `tune_stagger`, `tune_variance` and `tune_softness` take a `LivingScope` and do the same. New: the `Layer` trait and `Layered<L>`, `ClockOverride::full`, `flint_procgen::living::Segment` (another name for `BranchSegment`), and `flint_debug_ui::{KnobDesc, KnobSection, KnobControl, KnobValue}`. `Knob::ALL` now lists the knobs in the panel's order (`step` first). Each knob's hover in the panel says what it does. Frames, presets and `--report json` are byte-identical.
**What to do.** A game binary calling `set_echoes(c)` calls `tune_echoes(LivingScope::All, EchoOverride::full(c))` (variance likewise with `VarianceOverride::full`, stagger with `tune_stagger(LivingScope::All, s)`). Scenes and scripts: nothing.
