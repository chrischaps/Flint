### A frame's `gpu_ms` is its own GPU time, in the hitch log, captures and the frame history (#1104)
**What changed.** The GPU's time for a frame is read back a frame or two after it, and the frame history used to record it on the frame it arrived in, so a GPU-heavy hitch showed its GPU time on a later frame and a short one beside itself. Now each read-back carries the number of the frame it timed and lands on that frame's record: the hitch log's `gpu_ms`, a frame capture's `cpu.frame.gpu_ms` and `cpu.window[].gpu_ms` and the F2 graph's frames are each frame's own. The newest frame or two have `gpu_ms` `null` until theirs comes back. `--perf`'s smoothed GPU lines are unchanged. A game binary that read `SceneRenderer::take_gpu_frame_ms` reads `take_gpu_frame_times()` (`flint_render::GpuFrameTime { index, ms }`), and gives the timer its frame's number with `GpuTimer::set_frame_index`.
**What to do.** Nothing. A tool that compared a hitch line's `gpu_ms` with the frame after it can now read the hitch's own.
