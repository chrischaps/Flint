### Collision layers, masked `raycast`, `shape = "mesh"` / `"convex"`, and colliders that scale (#729)
**What changed.** A game names its layers in `flint.toml` (`[project.physics] layers = ["world", "player", "pickup"]`); `collider.layer` puts a collider on one and `collider.mask` lists what it meets, and two colliders touch, or a sensor fires, only when each one's mask holds the other's layer. `raycast(.., max_dist, mask)`, `raycast_2d(.., mask)` and `move_character(id, dx, dy, dz, mask)` take a layer's name or an array of names; `collision_layers()` lists them. The character controller meets what its collider's mask meets. `shape = "mesh"` is the entity's model's triangles (`collider.model` names another), `"convex"` their hull; a dynamic `mesh` is made convex. **A collider now takes its entity's world scale** (and its `offset` does too; a joint's `anchor` stays metres, unscaled); before, it was built unscaled with a warning.
**What to do.** A collider on a scaled entity whose `size` you wrote already scaled is now that much bigger: divide its `size` (and `offset`) by the scale. Colliders with no `layer` or `mask` behave as before.
