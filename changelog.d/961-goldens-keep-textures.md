### `flint goldens` uploads each texture and mesh once a run, and the shimmer cases draw 30 frames (#961)
**What changed.** In one process, `flint goldens` keeps the models' and image files' textures and the meshes' vertex and index buffers on the GPU device for the next case, as it keeps pipelines since #911. They are keyed by their bytes, so a changed file still uploads fresh. On WARP a case on a scene with several 2048-texel textures took 3 to 13 s just to upload them. The run's last line now ends `textures: N uploaded, M reused; mesh buffers: N uploaded, M reused`, and `--fresh-pipelines` uploads every case's anew too. While the cache is on, `TextureCache::update_rgba` refuses a kept texture. The stylize suite's two shimmer cases draw 30 frames, not 60; their means per frame are the same at any count from 20 up.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that parses the summary line should allow the new parts.
