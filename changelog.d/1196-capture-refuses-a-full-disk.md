### `flint capture` refuses a run that won't fit on the disk; `--no-disk-check` (#1196)
**What changed.** Before the first take, `flint capture` asks the disk under `out` for its free space and stops when the run's estimate (8 MB a 4K frame, scaled by area; with `--verify`, the takes plus the largest one's second run) passes 90% of it, plus whatever old takes the run replaces. The error names the estimate, the free space and the ways out: a `--scale` that fits, the largest `--shot`, `--out` and `--no-disk-check`. The header line now says the free space too, and `--dry-run` prints the same warning without refusing (`--json`: a `disk` block with `need_bytes`, `free_bytes`, `reclaimed_bytes` and `fits`).
**What to do.** Nothing. A run you know will fit (a disk that frees space as it goes) takes `--no-disk-check`.
