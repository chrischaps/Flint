### A script's translucent HUD colours draw darker: egui 0.36's blend (#958)
**What changed.** Script `draw_*` colours, text `stroke` and `glow` halos, world text and HUD sprites now premultiply their alpha as egui 0.36 does, in display values, like the rest of Flint's UI. Until now they kept egui 0.30's, which brightened every translucent light colour. A 50% white over black drew 188 and now draws 128, and a 0.3 cyan stroke turns dull blue. Opaque colours are unchanged, and so are dark translucent ones, such as shadows and black panels. Glows at 0.9 barely move. The `.style.toml` default `glow_color` is now `[1, 1, 1, 0.73]` (it was 0.5), and `flint init`'s HUD hints were retuned.
**What to do.** For a light colour that should look as before, raise its alpha to alpha^(1/2.2): 0.25 to 0.53, 0.3 to 0.58, 0.5 to 0.73, 0.75 to 0.88, 0.9 to 0.95. Leave dark colours alone. A colour over a ground as bright as itself can't be fully restored: brighten it. Table and measurements: the book's Upgrading the Engine page, HUD colours.
