### A player's own bus volumes, saved between runs: `set_user_bus_volume` / `user_bus_volume` (#1217)
**What changed.** Each bus has a second volume, the player's, which the mixer multiplies with the game's: `set_user_bus_volume("music", 0.4)` (0..1, clamped) under a script's `set_bus_volume("music", 0.5)` fade is heard at 0.2, and neither call overwrites the other. `user_bus_volume(bus)` reads it back on the same frame (1 when never set). The player saves it for the game, half a second after the last change, at a scene transition and on exit, in `<config>/<game id>/audio.toml` (`[buses] music = 0.4`; `<config>/flint/audio.toml` with no `[project] id`; `localStorage` `flint:/config/<id>/audio.toml` on the web; `audio.user.toml` in a `--save-dir`), and reads it at the next start before the first sound and `on_init`. `flint playtest` keeps it in memory. Nothing changes for a game that doesn't call them.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game that saved its own music and sfx levels through `persist_set` and re-applied them with `set_bus_volume` can move its settings menu to `set_user_bus_volume`, which frees `set_bus_volume` for fades.
