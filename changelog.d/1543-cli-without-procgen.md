### A `flint` built without `procgen` links no flint-procgen (#1543)
**What changed.** `procgen = false` under `[packages]` (or `flint-cli --no-default-features`) now leaves flint-procgen out of `flint` and the scene viewer too, not only the player. `flint gen <spec>`, `flint gen --list-generators` / `--describe`, `flint edit x.procgen.toml` and the hidden `gen-preview` / `tex-edit` still parse but exit 2 naming the `procgen` package. `flint gen <set>.glyphs.toml`, `flint edit x.terrain.toml` and `flint render --reference` work as before. Such a build also judges less: `flint check` holds no spec to its generator (`spec-param`, `bad-spec`) and checks `[wooze]` and `[living.species]` by their schema alone, and `render` and the viewer leave those tables unsettled, as the player without `living` reads neither. `flint_procgen::measure` is now `flint_import::measure` (`flint_procgen_ai::measure` still re-exports it).
**What to do.** Nothing for a game with procgen. A Rust caller of `flint_procgen::measure` names `flint_import::measure`.
