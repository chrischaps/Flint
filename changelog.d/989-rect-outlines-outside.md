### `draw_rect_outline` strokes outside its rect again, as before the egui 0.36 port (#989)
**What changed.** Since the egui 0.36 port (#945), a script's `draw_rect_outline` put half of its stroke inside the rect: a 4 px outline on a 100 x 40 box covered 8 to 112 across where it had covered 6 to 114, so an outlined box drew smaller and a thick outline crept over what was inside it. It is back outside the rect, where egui 0.30 drew it, measured against a wgpu 23 build pixel for pixel. The same goes for what a UI document outlines: a `rect` element with a `thickness`, and an element's `border_width`. Flint's own outlines (the scene tree's selection, the gradient editor's bar, the music guide and manifest map) are back outside too.

**What to do.** Nothing, for a HUD tuned before the port. A HUD tuned since it (on 0.36, from 14 November) whose outlines should sit where they sit now: move each outline in by half its thickness and shrink it by one thickness, or draw it as a filled frame.
