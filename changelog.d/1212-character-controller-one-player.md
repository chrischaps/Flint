### `character_controller`: `radius` and `camera_mode` are gone, a second controller is `second-player`, and a schema can retire a field (#1212)
**What changed.** `character_controller.radius` and `camera_mode` are no longer fields. Nothing ever read them: the capsule is the entity's `collider`, and a character's camera is always first person. A scene that still writes either gets `unknown-field` at load and in `flint check`, reading ``character_controller` has no field `camera_mode` any more``, with why in the hint. The `player` archetype no longer sets them. A scene with two entities carrying a `character_controller` (written or from an archetype) now says so: `second-player` on each after the first by name, naming the first as the player. Only the player's controller is read, as before. A schema can name the fields it took out under `[component.<name>.retired]` (`field = "why, and what to do"`), and a scene writing one is told that reason in place of the nearest field's name. An extension's `retired` adds to the earlier layer's. The controller's six action names (`move_forward`, `move_backward`, `move_left`, `move_right`, `jump`, `sprint`) stay fixed and are now constants in `flint_physics::character` (`ACTIONS`). ADR 0134 records why they, and the controller itself, stay as they are.
**What to do.** Delete `radius` and `camera_mode` from your scenes' and prefabs' `character_controller` tables; `flint check` lists each one. Give a second character no `character_controller`, and move it from a script with `move_character`.
