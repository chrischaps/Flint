### `flint render --scripts` runs every scene subsystem the player does (#1252)
**What changed.** A capture builds its scene's subsystems from the player's own list, so a subsystem added as one file in `player_app/subsystems/` draws in `flint render --time T --scripts` what `flint-player --frame-step` draws at T; before, only living props, the music and declared fields ran there, and any other was left out without a word. A subsystem that only a window can show declares it (`Subsystem::presentation_only`), and the capture names it once in its warnings. For a game embedding the engine's capture, `HeadlessGame::start_living(field, moment)` is now `start_subsystems(scene_file.scene_tables(), CaptureStart { wooze, moment, scripts })`, called at the same place.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless your own host drives `HeadlessGame`: call `start_subsystems` where it called `start_living`, before `start_scripts`.
