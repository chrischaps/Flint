### The renderer keeps bind groups over any depth view; `new_depth_id` and `depth_id` are gone (#960)
**What changed.** Bind groups over a host's depth view, and over the shadow map, fog map, style edges, albedo and pencil's images, are now kept by the objects themselves (wgpu 30 compares its handles by identity), not by ids and sizes standing in for them. A bare `&TextureView` handed to `encode_to` / `render_to` is now kept from frame to frame like `RenderContext::depth_target()`'s, where it used to make its groups every frame. `flint_render::new_depth_id`, `RenderContext::depth_id`, `HeadlessContext::depth_id` and `DepthKey::Frame` are removed; `DepthTarget::new` takes the view alone. A Rust style's `StyleFrame::edges_key` / `albedo_key` are replaced by `bind_over` / `depth_bind_over` with `edges_or` / `albedo_or`. Pictures are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing for scenes and scripts. A game binary that made its own depth texture and took a `new_depth_id()` for it drops that call and passes the view; one with a Rust style that keyed a bind group on `edges_key()` passes `&[frame.edges_or(&blank)]` to `depth_bind_over` instead.
