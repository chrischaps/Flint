### GPU pass times say "unavailable on this adapter" when its timestamps read zero (#1316)
**What changed.** When most of a frame's GPU scopes read exactly zero ticks for a whole 60-frame window (the Pixel 8 Pro's Mali under Chrome), the F2 overlay's GPU section, `?perf=1`'s panel and copied line, and `--perf`'s line say `GPU pass times unavailable on this adapter (N of M scopes read 0 ticks for 60 frames)` instead of listing `0.00`s; a trace's `gpu` track holds one mark of that name and its header a `gpu_pass_times` line; `--exit-after` warns under `[perf] avg GPU`. A game reads it as `RenderStats::gpu_timestamps` or `GpuTimer::timestamps()` (`flint_render::TimestampHealth`). Real pass times are shown as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that parses the perf line's `gpu` part should expect the new wording on such GPUs.
