### A style cap that leaves an entity out says so on screen, in the player and the scene viewer (#1246)
**What changed.** When a style's cap leaves something out (ASCII's 8 dissolve bands or 8 snap grids, pencil's and watercolor's paper images, the style mask's ids), the player and `flint edit`'s scene viewer now show a card in the bottom-left corner, above the style error card: `style 'ascii' leaves `prop008` out`, then the cap and how the entity draws instead. It is the warning the log already had (#740), and the card empties when a scene without drops loads. It is part of the debug HUD (`debug-hud`, on by default). `flint_debug_ui::paint_style_errors` now returns the height it drew, as `paint_script_errors` does.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game that draws `paint_style_errors` itself can stack `flint_debug_ui::paint_style_drops(ctx, &flint_render::stylize::variant_table::dropped(), inset)` above it.
