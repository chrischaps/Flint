### Pencil's `clutter` keeps a grass meadow from inking as a black band; `rotoscope` turns it on (#883)
**What changed.** Pencil takes `clutter` (0..1, default 0) in `[post_process.styles.pencil]`, in `pencil_style` and on `--style pencil:clutter=1`. At 1 a contour fades where the lines round it already cover most of a disc two outline widths across and of one three times that: a field of small silhouettes such as a `grass_ground` meadow, whose blades had merged into one black band from the near edge to the horizon. The meadow draws as a sheet with its near edge and horizon line; ordinary outlines keep theirs. At 0 every line is drawn as before, byte for byte. The `rotoscope` look now sets `clutter = 1`, so its drawings change slightly on thin features (a small figure's limbs lighten, about 1% of pixels on the props bench).
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a rotoscope scene relied on the old fill: set `clutter = 0` in its pencil table to get it back. For a meadow under another pencil look, add `clutter = 1`.
