### A `music_session` plays in the browser's worklet; `flint_music::clocked` replaces kira types in the suite's API (#1216)
**What changed.** On the web, a scene with a `music_session` no longer moves the whole mix to the main thread: its suite plays in the `AudioWorklet` with every other sound, and its beat lands on the same samples as on the desktop. If the worklet fails, the suite starts again on the main-thread fallback. In Rust, `AudioEngine::host` now hands its closure a `&mut dyn flint_audio::clocked::ClockedMixer` instead of kira's `AudioManager`, and `Unhosted::Worklet` is gone. `SuiteSession::start`, `BusMixer::build` and `ChartSession::open_shared` take that mixer, and an `AudioManager<B>` still coerces to it. `StemBus::set_gain`, `set_lpf`, `set_detune`, `set_sound_volume`, `set_alternate_mix` and `BusMixer::stop_all` take a `flint_music::clocked::Ramp` (`Ramp::now(secs)`, `Ramp::at(start, secs)`) instead of a kira `Tween`. A start time is a `clocked::Start` (`Now` or `Tick(n)`), so `SuiteSession::clock_time_at_raw` is now `start_at_raw`. `StemBus::play_cue` and `sound()` give a `clocked::StemVoice` (`set_volume(db, ramp)`, `set_rate(rate, ramp)`, `stop(ramp)`), and `SuiteSession::clock` is a `clocked::MixClock` (`ticks()`). `AudioEngine::mixer_epoch()` says when the mix has moved.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless your Rust drives a suite's buses with kira types. In that case, replace `Tween { start_time: StartTime::ClockTime(session.clock_time_at_raw(raw)), duration, .. }` with `Ramp::at(session.start_at_raw(raw), secs)`, and a `match` on `Unhosted::Worklet` with nothing.
