### `spline_mesh` sweeps any `profile`, a bad spline file is named by line and column, and `sweep_v1` makes swept models (#750)
**What changed.** A `spline_mesh` takes `profile = [[right, up], ...]` in place of `width` and `height` (a tube, an I-beam, a gutter), with `profile_closed = false` for an open surface and `smooth = true` for round shading; `width`/`height` are needed only without one. A `.spline.toml` with a mistake is skipped with a warning naming file, line and column (it said "Failed to parse"), and `flint check` reports it as `bad-spline`. A `position` of other than three numbers, a `spacing` of 0 and a wrong type are now errors where they were skipped or read as defaults. A striped chunked mesh's stripes run on across chunks instead of restarting. The new procgen generator `sweep_v1` makes one model from a profile and an inline path. `flint_player::spline_gen` is gone: `flint_render::load_scene_splines`.
**What to do.** Nothing for a track that loaded: FlintKart's render byte-identically. Run `flint check` on a scene whose spline was silently missing.
