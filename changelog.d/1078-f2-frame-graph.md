### F2 shows a frame-time graph with hitch marks and counters; `--stats=graph` (#1078)
**What changed.** The player's F2 overlay opens with a graph of the last four seconds of frames, each bar split into scripts, physics, other, render, hud/present and elsewhere, with the display's refresh period and twice it drawn across, and a vermillion mark over every hitch. Under it are the session's hitches, hitches a minute, the worst frame, the time since the last hitch and what it was (`last 100 ms: stall.rhai:on_update took 100`). F2 now steps through three states: the full overlay, the graph alone in a corner, then off. `flint-player --stats` still opens the full overlay; `--stats=graph` opens the graph alone (the value needs the `=`). While F2 shows anything, scripts are timed as under `--perf`. A game that embeds `PlayerApp` can set `app.capture.stats_view = flint_player::StatsView::Graph` beside `show_stats`. The widget is `flint_debug_ui::widgets::FrameGraph`.
**What to do.** Nothing. A test or capture script that pressed F2 twice expecting it closed now needs three presses.
