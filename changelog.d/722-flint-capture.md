### `flint capture <shots.toml>`: trailer and review footage from a shot list (#722)
**What changed.** A new command captures a shot list as deterministic frame sequences: each `[[shot]]` names a scene, `seconds`, and optionally `fps`, `width`/`height`, a `look`, a `seed`, an `input` script and `[[shot.take]]`s. Each take is one stepped run of `flint play`, written to `captures/<list>/<shot>/<take>/frame_NNNN.png` beside the list with a `take.json`. `--verify` captures each take twice and names every frame that differs, in pixels and levels, exiting 1 when they differ. `--encode mp4` writes an H.264 file a take when ffmpeg is on `PATH`, and `--dry-run --json` prints the plan. This replaces the showcase film's `tools/showcase/capture.py` and `compare.py`, which are gone; `tools/showcase/film.toml` is now a shot list (`[capture]`, `[[shot]]`). `flint init`'s `.gitignore` now ignores `captures/`.
**What to do.** Nothing. To film a game, write a shot list (the book's Filming a Showcase page; `showcase_game/trailer.shots.toml` is an example) and add `captures/` to your `.gitignore`.
