### The `vehicle` package ships an example: a test track, a playtest and a golden (#1629)
**Package.** vehicle
**What changed.** `packages/vehicle/examples/test_track/` is one small lap (a straight, a hairpin, a ramp and a patch of sand) on plain boxes, with a chase camera, a sand script that drives `vehicle_input`'s `grip_scale`, `speed_scale` and `accel_bonus`, and a playtest: `playtests/lap.input` plays a lap on a pad and `playtests/referee.rhai` fails the run when the kart leaves the track, flips or doesn't finish. CI plays it at 1/60, 1/30 and 1/144 s frames and holds the laps within 0.1 s (`playtests/lap_check.py`). `playtests/pilot.rhai` records a new lap when the handling is meant to change. The golden `vehicle-mid-corner` holds the kart posed mid-hairpin, and the book's Vehicles page shows it (Concepts, Vehicles, The Test Track).
**What to do.** Nothing. To see the package drive, `flint play packages/vehicle/examples/test_track/test_track.scene.toml` (from the engine's root, or `engine/` in a game). A change to the package that fails the lap is a regression, or a reason to record the lap again.
