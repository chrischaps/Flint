### The egui 0.36 port covers a game's tests: `egui_wants_keyboard_input`, `ModifiersChanged`, `FontsView::definitions`, frames that grow by their stroke (#996)
**What changed.** [Upgrading the Engine](https://docs.chaps.dev/guides/upgrading-the-engine.html#egui-030-to-036) was checked against a game's test targets as well as its binary. The table gains three rows that rustc names no replacement for: `wants_keyboard_input`, `wants_pointer_input`, `is_using_pointer` and `is_pointer_over_area` to their 0.36 names, `RawInput::modifiers` to an `Event::ModifiersChanged` (which egui keeps until the next one), and `Fonts::lock` to `FontsView::definitions`. A new behaviour change: since egui 0.31 a `Frame` lays out `2 × stroke.width` more than it did, so a framed panel fitted to a width comes out 2 px wider. A new Tests section says why a plain `run_ui` panics in a debug test (`Dropped TexturesDelta`).
**What to do.** A game porting its egui code: read the new rows. Run its tests' egui frames through `flint_debug_ui::run_headless_frame`, with flint-debug-ui as a dev-dependency. For 0.30's frame size, give a frame with a 1 px stroke `.outer_margin(Margin::same(-1))`.
