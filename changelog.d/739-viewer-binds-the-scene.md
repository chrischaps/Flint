### `flint edit` draws a scene's terrain, `[environment]`, shallow wooze and swaying grass (#739)
**What changed.** The scene viewer now binds a scene and prepares each frame the way the player and `flint render` do. On open and on each `--watch` reload it loads the scene's heightmap terrain (it drew none before, so a styled hillside was missing), masks a styled terrain, and sets the scene's `[environment]` levers (`shadows`, `default_lights`, ambient, wrap, Oren-Nayar, sheen), which it ignored. Shallow wooze meshes drift and terrain or woozy grass sways, on the viewer's own clock. An F4 lighting edit lasts until the next reload, which puts the scene's `[environment]` back. For hosts that embed the renderer: `SceneBinding::with_environment` hands `bind_scene` the scene's `[environment]`; `flint_scene::EnvironmentDef` is exported; the shallow wooze and woozy grass clocks are `flint_render::shallow_wooze::{ShallowWooze, GrassWooze}`.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that looked right only in the viewer because it ignored `[environment]` now looks as it does in the player.
