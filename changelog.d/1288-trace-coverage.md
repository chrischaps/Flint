### A script-heavy game's trace covers its whole look-back, and the readers say when one doesn't (#1288)
**What changed.** While the recorder records, a script's calls of an engine function (`get_field()`, `clamp()`) are no longer one scope a call: each function is one *summed* record a scope that called it, laid from the first call for their total, with `calls` and `worst_us` in its args. A call that recorded a scope inside it stays a scope of its own. A ring that wraps inside the window is now named in the header (`covered_from_ms`, `cut_threads`) and in the run's `[trace] wrote` line, with the `--trace-seconds` that would hold the window. `flint profile top`, `frame`, `explain`, `diff` and `flint frame open` read only the frames the scopes cover, say where they begin, and count a summed record as its calls; an older trace is read the same way from its `wrapped` header.
**What to do.** Nothing. A tool of your own that reads trace files should treat an event with a `calls` arg as a leaf (it may overlap its siblings) standing for that many calls.
