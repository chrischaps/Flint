### `--expression-listen` opens once the first frame is presented (#584)
**What changed.** The socket no longer listens from the moment the player starts. It binds at the start of the second frame, once the first has been presented, and says so in one log line under the new `listen` target, which the default filter and `--quiet` both keep: `[listen] expression: listening on 127.0.0.1:7777 from frame 2 (game time 0.017 s)`. Until then a connect is refused, so a client that has connected knows a state it sends is heard on the frame it arrives at, instead of states sent during startup (seconds, in a debug build) being drained together on the first frame. A malformed or non-loopback address still fails the run at start; a taken port now fails it when the socket opens, as an error under `listen`, and the player exits non-zero. The F3 Expression panel says "opens after the first frame" until it does.
**What to do.** A client that connects once and gives up on a refusal should retry until it connects, or wait for the `listen` line. A launcher that waited on a renderer log line plus a delay can drop both.
