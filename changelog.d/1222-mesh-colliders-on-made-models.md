### `shape = "mesh"` and `"convex"` colliders take procgen and runtime models' triangles (#1222)
**What changed.** A `mesh` or `convex` collider on a model a `*.procgen.toml` spec makes is the mesh the spec generated, the one drawn, where it was a box of `size` with a warning; one the deferred queue makes later becomes its hull on the frame after. On a runtime mesh (`model.runtime = true`) uploaded with `upload_mesh_with(.., MeshOptions { ray_target: true })`, the collider is the ray target's triangles from the frame after each upload, so `raycast()` meets what draws; before the first upload it is a box of `size`, now without a warning. A runtime mesh uploaded without `ray_target` keeps no triangles, and its collider says so once.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a scene leaned on the old box: a procgen or runtime model's `mesh`/`convex` collider is now its drawn shape, so its `size` no longer matters. To give a runtime mesh a collider, upload it as a ray target.
