### Fog reaches translucent draws at their own depth: echoes, glass and particles no longer seam at a horizon (#513)
**What changed.** Depth fog (`fog_enabled`, height fog, the aerial tint and ground fog) was drawn from the depth buffer, which blended meshes, living props' echoes and particles don't write, so each of their pixels was fogged by whatever lay behind it: a near echo in front of the far edge of a floor was fogged as the floor below the edge and not at all above it, with a hard line between. Now, while the fog is on and a translucent draw is in view, the renderer records what lies behind them and a "fog veil" of their depth and coverage, and fogs their own light at their own depth. A translucent draw near the camera in thick fog looks more like itself, and one far off is fogged as far. Frames with no fog, or no translucent draw in view, are drawn exactly as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that turned fog off to hide the seam (as chrischaps/Embodiment's spike did) can turn it back on.
