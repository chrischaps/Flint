### A `rotoscope` look, pencil's `grazing` key, and no more outline round a pencil frame (#404)
**What changed.** `--look rotoscope` (or `[post_process] look = "rotoscope"`) is a ninth built-in look: pencil lines on a blank white sheet, one layer of hatching only in the darks, boiling lines in the player. The pencil style takes `grazing` (0..1, default 0; also on `pencil_style`): at 1 a floor seen edge-on draws one horizon line instead of a thick dark band. Every pencil look used to ink a line round the edge of the frame; it no longer does, so a pencil render moves by a few pixels at its border and nowhere else. `layers = 0` is now cheaper (it skips the stroke-direction work), with the same picture.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a game pins a pencil render in its own goldens: re-bless it after checking that only the border moved. To keep a sheet blank under a floor, add `grazing = 1.0` to its `[post_process.styles.pencil]`.
