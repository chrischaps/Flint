### The `rotoscope` look shades by light and holds motion on twos (#887)
**What changed.** `--look rotoscope` now sets pencil's `tone = "light"` with `brightness = 0` (it was `tone = "picture"` at brightness 0.5), so a dark floor or costume in the sun stays blank paper and only shadows are hatched, on any scene. It also sets `[post_process] motion_hold = 12`: poses and positions are drawn on the same 12 ticks a second as the lines' boil, so animation under the look is on twos at 24 fps. Stills at time 0 are unchanged apart from the shading; physics and scripts still move smoothly.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that wants smooth motion under the look sets `motion_hold = 0` in its own `[post_process]` (the scene's keys win over a look it names) or passes `--motion-hold 0`. To get the old shading, set `tone = "picture"` and `brightness = 0.5` in `[post_process.styles.pencil]`.
