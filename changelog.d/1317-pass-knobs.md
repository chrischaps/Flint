### `--skip-pass` / `--repeat-pass` and `?skip_pass=` / `?repeat_pass=`: cost a part of the frame on any GPU (#1317)
**What changed.** `flint play`, `flint-player` and `flint render` take `--skip-pass <label>` and `--repeat-pass <label>:<N>`, and the web page takes `?skip_pass=` and `?repeat_pass=`. The labels are the ones F2 and `--gpu-time` print (`Scene`, `Style edges`, `Bloom`). A skipped part records no work and its targets keep what they last held. A repeated part draws N times inside its one timer scope. Parts that add into what they drew or keep history (`Grass`, `World lines`, `Selection outline`, `UI`, `ASCII`, a user style with a `history` target) draw once and print a warning. A game's Rust style can answer `StylePass::keeps_history` the same way. With neither knob set, nothing changes.
**What to do.** Nothing. To cost a pass where GPU timestamps read 0 (Mali), see the book's Profiling guide, "Costing a part without timestamps".
