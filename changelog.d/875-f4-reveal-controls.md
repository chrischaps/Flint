### F4 → Styles has a Reveal group for every style (#875)
**What changed.** Each style's section in F4 (and the player's F3 Styles panel) now has a **Reveal** group: a checkbox that switches the reveal on at `reveal = 1` (off takes the keys out and keeps them for the next time it goes on), the amount, a shape combo, the ink's colour picker and a slider for each `reveal_*` number, with the ranges and help of `--list-styles`. Copy TOML gives the keys back. **Commit to scene** now owns `blend` and every reveal key in the style's table, so one at its default or switched off in F4 is removed from the file. Before, a `blend` or `reveal` set in the file stayed there after a commit that no longer had it. Rust: `StyleSettings::replace_reveal(name, reveal)`, and `StyleDescriptor::table_keys()` begins with `RESERVED_KEYS`.
**What to do.** Nothing. Commit to scene now removes a `blend = 1.0` or a reveal key your table holds when F4 says the style has none.
