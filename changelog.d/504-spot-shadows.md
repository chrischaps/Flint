### A spot light casts a shadow with `cast_shadows = true` (#504)
**What changed.** `[entities.x.light]` takes `cast_shadows` (a bool, unset is false). On a spot light it draws a shadow map from the light, softened by its `source_radius` the way a sun's `angular_size` softens the cascades: sharp where a caster meets the floor, wider as they part. It works with or without a sun. One spot casts, the first by entity name that asks; another that asks lights without one and logs `shadow-cap`, as does a point light asking (no point shadows yet) and the strongest directional light saying `false` (it casts the cascades regardless). Meshes, skinned meshes and terrain take the shadow; grass and the ocean don't. The shadow map texture holds one more layer, 16 MB at the default 2048 texels. A scene that doesn't ask renders as it did. Example: `examples/spot_shadow.scene.toml`.
**What to do.** Nothing. To give a key light its contact shadow, add `cast_shadows = true` to its spot, and drop any directional light you placed along it only for its shadow.
