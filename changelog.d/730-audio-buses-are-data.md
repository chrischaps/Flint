### A game's own mixer buses in `flint.toml`; `set_bus_volume(name, v)`; `Bus::SFX` / `Bus::MUSIC` (#730)
**What changed.** Buses are data: `sfx` and `music` always, plus any a game declares in `flint.toml` as `[audio.buses.<name>]` with `parent` (another bus, or `master`) and `volume`. They mix the same on the desktop and in the browser's worklet. `audio_source.bus`, a script's `bus` option and the new script function `set_bus_volume("voice", 0.5)` take any declared name; an unknown one is warned about once (a sound plays on `sfx`). In Rust, `flint_audio::Bus` is now a bus number: `Bus::Sfx` / `Bus::Music` are deprecated constants for one release (use `Bus::SFX` / `Bus::MUSIC`), and `Bus::from_name` gives way to `ctx.audio.bus(name)` / `AudioEngine::bus`. `AudioEngine::manager_mut()` is gone: start clocked content with `AudioEngine::host(|manager| ..)`. `--music-volume` / `--sfx-volume` now scale the volume `flint.toml` starts those buses at.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless you called `manager_mut` (use `host`) or want more buses (declare them in `flint.toml`). Rename `Bus::Sfx` / `Bus::Music` before the next release.
