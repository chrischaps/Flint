### Sprites play through `animator`: `sprite_animator` is retired, and flipbooks get sequences and cues (#1620)
**Package.** flipbook
**What changed.** Sprite-sheet clips are the new `flipbook` package's (standard; `flipbook = false` drops them), played on animation's clock through the `animator` every clip uses (ADR 0139). `sprite_animator`'s `clip`, `playing` and `speed` are retired for one release: a scene that still writes them animates as before (frame for frame) and hears ``sprite_animator` has no field `clip` any more`` from `flint check` and on load. `animated_sprite` is `transform`, `sprite`, `animator`. `play_sprite`, `stop_sprite`, `set_sprite_speed` and `is_sprite_playing` keep their names in the `flipbook` module, and now: `play_sprite` on a paused clip resumes it (a finished one-shot still replays), `stop_sprite` holds the frame, `speed` takes `-10..10` (negative plays backwards), and sprites take `autoplay`, `loop` and `sequence`. A sequence's cues reach `on_sequence_cue` on a sprite, and `on_animation_end(clip)` fires for a one-shot of any kind, so a 3D script defining it now hears skeletal one-shots too.
**What to do.** Rename `[entities.<name>.sprite_animator]` to `[entities.<name>.animator]` before the next release removes the alias.
