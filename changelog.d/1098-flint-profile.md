### `flint profile` reads a scope trace from the command line (#1098)
**What changed.** A new command reads the trace `flint-player --trace`, `--trace-on-hitch` and Shift+F3 write, with no GPU, scene or schemas. `flint profile explain <trace> [frame]` says why a frame was slow in its first line, holding it against the trace's normal frames: `frame 525 took 107.28 ms (median 4.57): stall.rhai:on_update 100.09 ms against its median 0.032, ...`, or a scope that `ran only here`, or the phase when no scope covers the time. `flint profile frame <trace> <n|worst|hitch>` prints that frame's scope tree, `flint profile top <trace>` the scopes by total, self time, worst call or calls (`--thread`, `--frames a..b`), and `flint profile diff <a> <b>` two traces' per-scope mean and p90 deltas, marking the ones the frames' own spread can explain. Every view answers `--json`.
**What to do.** Nothing. After a hitch, `flint profile explain` on the file `--trace-on-hitch` wrote is the quickest way to its cause.
