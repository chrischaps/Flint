### Point and spot lights light grass and water (#1048)
**What changed.** A `point` or `spot` light now reaches grass (`grass_ground` and a terrain's grass) and the ocean and water bodies, as it reaches meshes and terrain: a lantern in a meadow lights the blades around it, and a lamp over water lays a streak of cel glints and a little scattered light on the waves. Before, both read only the sun, so a lamp's pool stopped at the grass line and the waterline. The casting spot's shadow (`cast_shadows = true`) falls on both too. A scene with no point or spot light draws exactly as before; one with lamps near grass or water gets brighter there.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that leaned on the old hole (a lamp placed by water or grass and tuned bright to make up for it) may want its `intensity` turned down.
