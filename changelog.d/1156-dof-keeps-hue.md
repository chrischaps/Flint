### Depth of field under `chromatic_aberration` no longer rings bright surfaces in green (#1156)
**What changed.** While `chromatic_aberration` was above 0.001, the composite took green from the picture after `dof_strength` and `radial_blur` had blurred it, but read red and blue from the sharp source. Wherever a blur spread a bright surface, green spread and red and blue didn't, so emissive edges, flames and hard-lit edges out of focus got a green halo. An aberration far too faint to see caused it: a script that eases `set_chromatic_aberration` down by `exp(-k t)` and stops calling it leaves a small value behind, and the value stays. Red and blue now go through the same blurs at their offsets. Frames with depth of field and radial blur both off, or with no aberration, are byte-identical. A frame with both on costs the depth-of-field gather three times.
**What to do.** Nothing. If you dropped `set_dof` because of the ring, you can put it back. A script that eases the aberration out should end with `set_chromatic_aberration(0.0)`, so it doesn't pay for the extra gathers.
