### A `ui_text` font follows a scene transition into another folder (#312)
**What changed.** `ui_text.font = "<name>"` is found by the project's search from the scene's folder, but the renderer kept the first font a name loaded, along with its uploaded atlas (`font:<name>`). A game with `levels/town/fonts/pixel.glyphs.toml` and `levels/dungeon/fonts/pixel.glyphs.toml` drew the town's `pixel` in the dungeon after a transition. `SceneRenderer::load_ui_font` now remembers the scope its fonts came from: a load from another scope forgets them and their textures, and the next scene reads its own. Within one scene, and across scenes in the same folder, nothing reloads. The styles' glyph sets and palettes (the ASCII style's `font`) already followed the scene, since the player hands the renderer each scene's `AssetScope`. `SceneRenderer::loaded_ui_font(name)` is new: the font a scene's `ui_text` draws with, if it loaded.
**What to do.** Nothing.
