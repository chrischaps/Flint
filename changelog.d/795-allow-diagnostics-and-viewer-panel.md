### `[project.diagnostics] allow` in flint.toml, and the viewer lists a scene's diagnostics (#795)
**What changed.** A project can accept a diagnostic by its code, everywhere or at one place: `allow = ["unknown-field:material.wetness", "unknown-table"]` under `[project.diagnostics]`. What it names is left out of `flint validate`, `flint check`, every load's summary and the viewer, and each says how many (`2 warnings (1 allowed by flint.toml)`; `"allowed"` in the JSON). An entry naming no code is refused when the manifest is read, as is any other key of `[project.diagnostics]`. `flint edit <scene>` now lists the scene's diagnostics in its status panel, refreshed on every hot reload; clicking a row selects its entity. `[project] strict` is still not applied by a load (now documented): it changes what `flint validate`, `flint check` and the viewer report, not what the player logs.
**What to do.** Nothing. To quiet a warning your game means, such as a field only scripts read, either declare the field with `extend = true` (#794) so it is checked, or allow the code here so it is not.
