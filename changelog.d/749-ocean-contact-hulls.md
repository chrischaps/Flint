### `ocean_contact` foams every hull where it is drawn; `ocean.show_probe` is gone (#749)
**What changed.** Contact foam reads each `ocean_contact` entity's world transform, so a hull under a parent or posed with `rotation_quat` (physics write-back) foams where it is drawn, and its half-extents grow with world scale. Up to four foam at once, the nearest the camera past that, and each one left out is warned about once by name (it used to be the first found, at its local position and Euler yaw). A dynamic body's velocity comes from physics. A lone upright hull renders byte-identically: Float's scenes are unchanged. The `ocean` component's `show_probe` field is removed. The Ocean Debug panel's **Diagnostics → Wave probe** box now draws the parity cross itself, so no scene entity or script is needed.
**What to do.** If your scene sets `show_probe`, delete it (an unknown field now draws a warning), along with any buoy entity and script that read it. Use the panel's Wave probe instead. A second hull just needs its own `ocean_contact`.
