### `engine/` is 169 MB instead of 390 MB; `testModels/` and some example textures are gone or renamed (#759)
**What changed.** The engine tree a game carries as `engine/` lost its duplicates and its 4K textures (ADR 0122). `testModels/` is deleted: its models were copies of `examples/models/`, and `DoubleSidedCard.glb` is now `tests/fixtures/models/`. In `examples/`, `textures/wood_planks.png` is now `textures/wood_planks.jpg` (2048 texels, was 4096), `wooden_bench.glb` and `Lantern.glb` carry 2048-texel JPEG colour maps, `rolling_meadow/` uses `terrain/`'s four layer textures, and `worn_planks_diff_4k.jpg`, the Poliigon normal, roughness, displacement, AO and metallic maps, `mug2.glb` and `CubeWithTexture.glb` (read by no scene) are deleted. The book's PNGs are recompressed, same pixels. The engine's CI now fails a texture over 2048 texels or 3 MB, a file over 8 MB, or one binary tracked twice.
**What to do.** If a game names `engine/testModels/...`, `engine/examples/textures/wood_planks.png` or one of the deleted files, point it at the file above, or copy the old one into the game. To keep `engine/examples`, `engine/docs`, `engine/tests` and `engine/showcase_game` out of a game's working tree (166 MB to 41 MB), use the sparse checkout in the game-project guide.
