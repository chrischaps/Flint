### `form_v1` rows drift along their lines: `row_pace`, `row_pace_spread`, `[living] row_rate` (#576)
**What changed.** A `form_v1` spec with `card_layout = "rows"` takes `row_pace` (metres a second, default 0) and `row_pace_spread` (0 to 1, default 0.3): the words travel along their lines, slide out under one margin and back in under the other, each line at its own pace. They read a row clock that a new run-time knob, `row_rate` (0 to 8, unset 1), runs faster or slower: a scene's `[living] row_rate`, `[living.species.<spec>] row_rate`, a preset, the F3 panel's row rate slider, or `tune_living(#{ living: #{ row_rate: r } }[, seconds])`. A new rate moves no word on the frame it's set. Echoes show the lines a little before or after now. `flint render --report json` adds each prop's `row_time` and its `row_rate` value. At `row_pace = 0` nothing changes: no bones are added and every spec draws as before. Example: `examples/drifting_page.scene.toml`.
**What to do.** Nothing. To drift a page's writing, add `row_pace` to its `[params]`.
