### `transition = "dissolve"` turns exactly `blend` of a prop's cells, outline slivers included (#554)
**What changed.** An ASCII prop that dissolves (`ascii_style { transition = "dissolve" }`) ranks each of its cells in the dissolve order and turns those ranked below `blend` (#398). Before, a cell counted as the prop's only when one of its 16 sample points landed on the prop, so a thin edge or an outline sliver between them took the unranked order, and a prop could turn a cell or two more or fewer than its share (a barrel of about 40 cells at blend 0.5 turned 47-56% of them over 17 placements). Now a cell counts when any of its pixels is the prop's, so the same barrel turns exactly half at every placement. A prop with `silhouette = "cell"` is unchanged: its cells are still the ones that cover `cell_share` of the samples. A dissolve part-way through can show a cell or two differently at its edge; blend 0 and 1 are the same.
**What to do.** Nothing. A golden of a dissolving prop may need re-blessing.
