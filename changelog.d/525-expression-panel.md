### F3 shows an Expression panel when the player has an expression feed (#525)
**What changed.** A run with `--expression-timeline` or `--expression-listen` now gets an **Expression** panel among the F3 panels (builds with `debug-hud`, the default). It shows the latest `expression()` state as a key/value table with its source and how long ago it arrived in game time, the last 12 states newest first with what changed in each, the timeline's playhead and next event, and the socket's open connections and the lines it dropped (malformed, oversize) and the states it dropped under a flood. A state shows in the panel on the frame it is delivered. F3 now has a panel to open in a scene that had none, when a feed is running. Nothing changes for a run without a feed.
**What to do.** Nothing. Press F3 while rehearsing a timeline or driving the socket to see what scripts hear without `RUST_LOG=expression=info`.
