### Motion on twos: a `motion_hold` component, `[post_process] motion_hold` and `--motion-hold` (#405)
**What changed.** `[entities.x.motion_hold] rate = 12` draws an entity and its descendants on ticks, 12 a second: its drawn position and a skinned model's pose change only on each tick and hold in between, the way rotoscoped film holds its drawings. Only the drawing is held; physics, IK and scripts still see smooth motion. A style's `boil` steps on the same ticks, so `boil = 12` redraws pencil lines in the frames the pose moves. `[post_process] motion_hold = 12`, or `--motion-hold 12` on `flint render`, `flint play` and `flint-player`, holds the whole frame, the post time styles read included. Scripts: `set_motion_hold(id, rate)`, `clear_motion_hold(id)`, `get_motion_hold(id)`. A game embedding `SceneRenderer` with its own loop calls `set_motion_time(seconds)` each frame before uploading bones, or nothing is held.
**What to do.** Nothing; scenes without the component draw as before. Example: `examples/motion_hold.scene.toml`; book: concepts/stylize-passes.md, "Motion on twos".
