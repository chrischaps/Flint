### `--perf-report <file.json>` and `scripts/perf-compare.py`: perf numbers as data (#1093)
**What changed.** `flint play` and `flint-player` take `--perf-report FILE` beside `--exit-after`: at exit they write what the `[perf]` lines print as JSON (`format = "flint-perf-report"`, version 1), plus each phase's and script's mean and max over the run, the GPU's passes as spreads, hitch counts, and the machine (adapter, backend, present mode, size, build profile, engine commit). The lines are printed from the same numbers. It records every frame, times scripts and turns GPU timing on, as `--hitch-log` and `--perf` do. `flint render --gpu-time-json` now also writes `format`, `version`, `build_profile` and `engine_commit`; its pass fields are unchanged and shared with the report. `scripts/perf-compare.py a.json b.json` prints each metric's change against a noise floor, refuses reports from different setups, and exits 1 when frame p50 regresses past `--tolerance`. `scripts/perf-run.sh <scene>` runs the player five times with vsync off and keeps the least noisy report.
**What to do.** Nothing. To compare a change: `bash scripts/perf-run.sh <scene> --out before.json`, change, again with `--out after.json`, then `python scripts/perf-compare.py before.json after.json`.
