### Spot lights light what they point at; lights say what they drop; `inner_angle_deg` / `outer_angle_deg` (#682)
**What changed.** Spot lights on meshes were evaluated backwards, so one pointing down lit nothing below it: they now light the way their `direction` says, which brightens any scene with a spot light. A light with no `direction` shines down its entity's -Y and turns with it and its parents (an unturned directional light with no `direction` is now a sun overhead, not one below the ground). Past the shader's 16 point or 8 spot lights, the ones nearest the camera are drawn, not the first by name, and each light left out is named once (`light-cap`); an unknown or missing `type` is said by entity name too. Spot cones take degrees as `inner_angle_deg` / `outer_angle_deg`; the radian keys still read as before.
**What to do.** Look at scenes with spot lights: they are brighter now, so check their `intensity`. Write new cones in degrees.
