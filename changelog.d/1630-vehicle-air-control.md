### A vehicle steers and keeps its speed in the air, and a drift slides its tyres (#1630)
**Package.** vehicle
**What changed.** `vehicle` has two new fields. `air_control` (0 to 1, default 0) is the share of the yaw assist that still steers with fewer than two wheels down, about the world's up, so a hop or a jump can turn; with no wheel down the travel turns with the nose by the same share, so it lands going where it points rather than sideways. `air_drag` (0 to 1, default 1) is the share of `drag` kept with no wheel down; 0 is an arcade kart whose drag balances its drive, so a hop costs no speed. And while a game holds a `slip_target` (a drift), `grip_scale` now scales the tyres' side friction too, so a nose held off its travel isn't braked by four tyres gripping sideways. Without a slip target the tyres grip as before. The Vehicles panel has an `air_control` slider. FlintKart's karts, now on the package, set `air_control = 1` and `air_drag = 0`.
**What to do.** Nothing: the defaults drive as before, and the test track's lap is unchanged. An arcade game whose hop should steer and keep its speed sets `air_control = 1.0` and `air_drag = 0.0` on its `vehicle`. A game that drifts with `slip_target` and a `grip_scale` under 1 will find its drift keeps more speed; raise `grip_scale` to have it back.
