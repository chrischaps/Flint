### Per-entity styles: echoes now veil, and woozy meshes are masked where they're drawn (#737)
**What changed.** A per-entity style (ASCII, pencil, a user style reading the mask) handles two more cases correctly. A living prop's echoes in front of a styled entity now unmask it by their alpha, as glass and particles already did (#290). Before, the style drew the echoes as part of the entity behind them, so leaves in front of a glyph wall became glyphs, ink outlines included. A styled mesh with `shallow_wooze` is now masked where it was drawn. Before, its mask stayed at the rest pose, leaving unstyled holes where the mesh had drifted off and styling the pixels it had drifted away from. Billboards were already right. Scenes with neither case render byte for byte as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A styled scene with echoes in front of a styled entity, or with a styled woozy mesh, looks different. Check those shots, or re-bless their goldens if a game keeps any.
