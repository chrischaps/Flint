### `flint gen --describe` lists the keys of nested tables (#1173)
**What changed.** `--describe` (text and `--markdown`) and the flint-procgen README catalog now print a nested table's keys under the key that holds it: `creature_v1`'s `bones[].name`, `body_parts[].shape`, `limb_chains[].segments[].length` and `materials.<name>.roughness`, and `sweep_v1`'s `control_points[].twist`, each with its type, its default and what it does. A key a table must have reads `required`. The creature schema now also lists keys its parser always read but the schema never named (`body_parts[].offset`, `limb_chains[].attach_offset` and `.material`, `segments[].rest_angles`), and holds a segment to `length`, `radius_start` and `radius_end`, which generation already refused to go without.
**What to do.** Nothing.
