### `flint script check` reports a `global::` read that would fail every frame: `unknown-global` (#1007)
**What changed.** `global::NAME` reaches a script's top-level `const`s, and nothing else. Between #997 and #1033 every such read failed in callbacks; #1033 fixed that, and this adds the check that tells the two apart before the game runs. A `global::NAME` read in a script with no top-level `const NAME` is now the error `unknown-global` in `flint script check`, `flint check` and `flint validate`, at its line and column. The hint names a top-level `let` of the same name, which `global::` never reaches, or the nearest constant (did you mean `KEYS`?). Modules aren't judged, since a module's `global::` refers to its own constants. Float's 16 scripts, with 41 `global::` reads, pass.
**What to do.** If the check now fails on a script, make the value a `const` if it never changes, or read it by its bare name in the callback. Until that's fixed, the read fails every time it runs.
