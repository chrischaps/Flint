### `flint edit` says what it says through the player's log: one load summary, `--log-format`, `--quiet` (#1164)
**What changed.** The editor windows (`flint edit` on a scene, model, procgen spec, terrain, texture pipeline or particle effect) write every line through `tracing` to stderr, as `flint play` does; none prints to stdout any more. A scene, in the viewer or with `--spline`, says its load in one line once its models are in, and again after each hot reload (`Loaded <scene>: N entities, 4 models in 180 ms; no warnings`), in place of `Loaded scene:` / `Entities:` and a line for each file. The window's notices (saves, reloads, undo, F4 toggles) are info lines of target `viewer`. `flint edit` takes `--log-format text|json` and `--quiet`, and the model previewer's per-cue `[sequence] cue` line is debug. A `--render` still still answers `Rendered ...` on stdout.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a script read the viewer's stdout: read stderr, or `--log-format json` and the `load` line's `scene`, `entities`, `warnings`, `errors` and `ms` fields. `RUST_LOG=load=debug` lists each file again.
