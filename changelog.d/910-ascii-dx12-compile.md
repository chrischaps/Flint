### The `ascii` style compiles in seconds on DX12 again (#910)
**What changed.** Since #654, a game drawing the `ascii` style on DX12 waited about a minute in a release build (25 s in a debug one) the first time ASCII drew: FXC, DX12's shader compiler, inlined the per-pixel corner test about 45 times into the glyph shader. Each edge cell's glyph is now decided once per cell, in two small passes whose texel the glyph pass and the edge debug view (`--debug-mode edges`) read. Under FXC the glyph shader now compiles in about 6 s (9 s before the corners), and the two new passes in under a second; `flint render --style ascii` on DX12 went from 30 s to 11 s on an RTX 4070. Frames are byte-identical: the same glyphs, corners and colours.
**What to do.** Nothing. A user style (`*.wgsl`) that calls a neighbour-reading helper per pixel can hit the same wall on DX12: decide per cell in a pass of its own.
