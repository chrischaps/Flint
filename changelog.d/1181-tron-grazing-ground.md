### `tron` keeps a ground seen at a grazing angle as wire, not solid glow (#1181)
**What changed.** The `tron` style's depth edges now fire where the depth bends (the second difference of 1 / distance), not wherever it changes, so a flat road, floor or sea running to the horizon no longer fills with glow from a few tens of metres out; objects keep their outlines, now visible against it. The world grid's lines thin and fade toward the horizon instead of breaking into dashes. The `edges`, `grid` and other settings keep their meaning: a step of 5% of the view distance is still a full edge. A frame under `tron` that shows a ground plane looks different.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that only blended `tron` in while the camera faced a wall to hide the glow can show it over open ground now.
