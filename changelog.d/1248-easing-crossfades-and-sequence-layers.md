### Crossfades and sequence layer fades take an easing: `animator.blend_easing`, `blend_to(.., easing)`, a step's `easing` (#1248)
**What changed.** A crossfade's weight can now move as one of the engine's easings (`linear`, `smooth`, `ease_in`, `ease_out`, `ease_in_out`) instead of only linearly: write `blend_easing` on the `animator`, call `blend_to(entity, clip, seconds, easing)`, or give a `.sequence.toml` `blend` step an `easing`. The easing stays written, so later crossfades ease the same way. A sequence's `layer` step takes `easing` too, written as the layer's `fade_easing`. A step naming an easing that doesn't exist is a load error naming the file and the step. An animator that never writes `blend_easing`, and a step with no `easing`, fade linearly as before.
**What to do.** Nothing.
