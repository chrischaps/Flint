### A bus ducks under another, and every bus has its own low-pass: `[audio.buses.<name>] duck` and `lowpass`, Rhai `set_bus_lowpass` (#1218)
**What changed.** A bus in `flint.toml` takes `duck = { under = "voice", by_db = -9, attack = 0.08, release = 0.6 }` (only `under` is required): while anything plays on `voice` (or a bus under it) the bus dips by `by_db` and comes back after, on the desktop and in the browser's worklet alike. Every bus, `sfx` and `music` included, has its own low-pass: `lowpass = 3000` starts one muffled, and scripts call `set_bus_lowpass("sfx", 800)` (`20000` opens it; extensions `ctx.audio.set_bus_lowpass(bus, hz)`). An open bus low-pass costs nothing. The worklet's `Command::BusNew` carries the bus's cutoff, and there is a new `Command::BusLowpass`; `MixerBackend::add_bus` takes the cutoff. Games without the new keys sound as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game that faded its music by hand under every line of dialogue can declare a `duck` instead.
