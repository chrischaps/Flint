### The player captures frames: `--capture-frame-at`, `--capture-on-hitch`, Shift+F2 and `capture frame` (#1086)
**What changed.** `flint-player` and `flint play` write the frame debugger's capture from a running game. `--capture-frame-at <secs>` (repeatable) captures at a game time, `--capture-on-hitch[=N]` the frame after each of the first N hitches, Shift+F2 the next frame, and `<t> capture frame` in an `--input-script` the frame at that line. Each is a `<scene>-t<secs>.flintframe/` folder under `--capture-dir`, or `captures/` at the project's root. It holds `flint render --frame-capture`'s manifest and images, plus the HUD's `UI` pass, the frame history around the frame (and the hitch), and `replay/` (the world, the frame's inputs, bone palettes, particles, assets by hash). The writing happens on a thread, and the capturing frames aren't counted as hitches. The manifest gains `trigger`, `scene`, `engine`, `cpu`, `replay` and `notes` (version 1 still). Desktop only.
**What to do.** Add `/captures/` to your game's `.gitignore` (new projects have it): a capture holds renders of your game. Shift+F2 is taken by the player.
