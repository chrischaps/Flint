### The living package has its own folder, and the player, the CLI and the viewer name none of it (#1558)
**What changed.** `flint-living` and `flint-wooze` moved to `packages/living/crates/`. What the player, `flint render`, `flint check`, `flint gen --strip` and the scene viewer did behind their `living` features is now flint-living's: its `player` part (`flint_living::player::PACKAGE`), its `debug-hud` part (the Living Props panel, which moved out of flint-debug-ui), and its `cli` part. These reach the engine through the Package API's registration points. Every flag and table reads as before, and every grove, wooze and echo render tested here is byte-identical to one from before the move. A package manifest may now hold `[with.<package>] features`, for a part that needs another package listed too. `flint-viewer` has no `living` feature now, and `flint-debug-ui` no features at all.
**What to do.** A game binary that called `app.compare_living_at_start(a, b, split)` sets `app.launch_flags_mut()` instead: `flags.set("compare", "a,b")`, plus `flags.set("split", "true")` to start split. One that read `app.scene.living` / `app.scene.wooze` reads `[living]` / `[wooze]` from `app.scene.tables`, which `apply_scene_settings` now keeps settled. One that named `flint_player::{living_wanted, wooze_of_scene, boil_at, species_problems, apply_living_preset, living_import}` uses `flint_living::player::props::*` and `flint_procgen::player::import_of`. `HeadlessGame::make_assets` takes the scene's tables first. `CaptureStart` has no `wooze` field: a capture's wooze field goes in through `HeadlessGame::resources_mut()`. Otherwise nothing changes.
