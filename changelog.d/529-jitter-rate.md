### Living Props: `jitter_rate` sets how fast the boil ticks, live (#529)
**What changed.** A new knob, `jitter_rate`, runs a living prop's leaf-card and shade boil faster or slower than its spec's `jitter.step`: 2 ticks twice as often, 0.5 half, 0 holds it, up to 8. It layers like `softness`: `[living] jitter_rate`, `[living.species.<spec>] jitter_rate`, a `.wooze.toml` preset, the F3 panel's Clock section, `tune_living(#{ living: #{ jitter_rate: 2.0 } })` (eased with `tune_living(map, seconds)`), `clear_living(species, ["jitter_rate"])` and `--report json`'s values. A change never pops: each prop's jitter clock moves on at the new rate from where it stands, so every card holds until its next tick, and echoes follow. The pose clock (`step`) is untouched. Unset is 1, and a scene that never sets it renders byte for byte as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. To quicken a form's flicker with activity, tune `jitter_rate` rather than `jitter` (which is how far it moves, not how fast).
