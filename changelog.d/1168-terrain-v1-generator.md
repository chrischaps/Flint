### A terrain spec is a `terrain_v1` procgen spec: `flint gen x.terrain.toml --register`, `--ai-generator terrain_v1` (#1168)
**What changed.** A new generator, `terrain_v1`, wraps the terrain bake, and `flint gen` reads a `.terrain.toml` as a spec for it, so every procgen flag works on terrain: `--register` stores and reuses both maps, `--batch`, `--validate`, `--print-spec` (the recipe as a `*.procgen.toml`), `--describe terrain_v1`, and the AI steps (`--ai-create "rolling hills" --ai-generator terrain_v1`; words such as hills, mountains and valley pick it). The maps are written as an image set: `-o terrain/meadow.png` makes `terrain/meadow_height.png` (16-bit grey, the same bytes as before) and `terrain/meadow_splat.png`, where #719's route made `meadow.png` and `meadow_splat.png`; `-o dir/` makes `output_height.png` and `output_splat.png`. A value the bake can't use (a splat rule's `layer` past 3, a resolution over 4096) is now an error that stops the run, not a warning, and the `--json` document is the procgen one (`variants[].files`), without #719's `coverage`. The terrain editor lists the spec's warnings under its Save buttons. Procgen's `ImageData` gained `format` (`PixelFormat::Rgba8` or `L16`) and `ChannelSemantics::Splat`; the procgen disk cache's files from before are misses and are regenerated once.
**What to do.** A script or scene that names the heightmap `flint gen` wrote as `<name>.png` names `<name>_height.png` instead. Rust that builds an `ImageData` literal adds `format: PixelFormat::Rgba8`.
