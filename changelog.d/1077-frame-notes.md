### Each recorded frame keeps notes of what the engine did on it, and a hitch names them as its cause; `flint_core::frame_notes` (#1077)
**What changed.** Model and texture loads, pipeline and style builds, scene transitions, chunk loads, script loads and hot reloads, procgen, audio decodes, surface reconfigures, frames of several physics steps and clock stalls now leave a note (kind, detail, ms) on the frame record they happened in. A hitch keeps its frame's notes and the frame before's, and `likely_cause()` names them or says `no engine event`. Nothing shows them yet (#1078, #1079). New API: `flint_core::frame_notes` (`note`, `timed`, `begin`, `listen`, `take`), a per-thread channel the player drains once a frame. A note where no one listens costs a flag read. `pipeline_cache::timed` is crate-private and now takes the label.
**What to do.** Nothing.
