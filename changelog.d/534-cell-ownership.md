### Whole-frame ASCII's `silhouette = "cell"` snaps another style's entity to whole glyphs (#534)
**What changed.** With ASCII on for the whole frame and `silhouette = "cell"` in its table (`--style ascii:silhouette=cell`), the edge of an entity in another style (a `style_mask { style = "pencil" }` prop) no longer cuts glyph cells in half. Each cell along it is a whole glyph, when at least `cell_share` of it is not the entity's, or wholly the entity's, its other pixels showing the plain scene. This holds in either chain order. Before, the option snapped only ASCII's own per-entity props, and every other style's edge was cut pixel by pixel. With `silhouette = "pixel"` (the default) every frame is byte-identical to before. A Rust style can ask for the same cut by returning its grid from the new `StylePass::ownership_cells`.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene whose ASCII table already says `silhouette = "cell"` now draws its other styles' props with stepped, whole-cell edges. Lower `cell_share` to give more edge cells to the glyphs.
