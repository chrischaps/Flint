### A reveal's tear can read as torn paper: `reveal_rim`, `reveal_rim_color`, `reveal_shadow` (#876)
**What changed.** Every style's reveal takes three more keys. `reveal_rim` (points, default 0) lays a band of bare paper on the style's side of the line, ragged and frayed as a torn sheet's white core is; `reveal_rim_color` sets its colour (unset: the style's `paper` colour lifted halfway to white). `reveal_shadow` (0 to 1, default 0) darkens the live side under the torn edge, longest below and to the right. All three work in `[post_process.styles.<name>]`, `--style pencil:reveal=0.5,reveal_rim=3,reveal_shadow=0.4`, `--sweep`, `set_post_style_param` and `tween_post_style_param`. At their defaults a reveal draws exactly as before. The three names are now reserved: a user style may not name a parameter or option `reveal_rim`, `reveal_rim_color` or `reveal_shadow`.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a user style of yours uses one of those names; rename it.
