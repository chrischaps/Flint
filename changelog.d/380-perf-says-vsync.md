### `--perf` and `?perf=1` say when GPU times were taken under vsync (#380)
**What changed.** The `flint-player --perf` line, the web page's `?perf=1` panel and `--exit-after`'s `[perf] avg GPU` line now name the swapchain's present mode (`present fifo`, `fifo-relaxed`, `mailbox` or `immediate`) and say `under vsync` or `no vsync` beside the GPU time. Under vsync, when the GPU was busy for less than three quarters of the wall clock, they add `at idle clocks`: a GPU that waits for the display clocks down, and its pass times read high (1.4x on an RTX 4070 laptop, about 5x on DX12 in #270). `RenderContext::present_mode_in_use()` and `flint_render::resolve_present_mode` give the mode a host's surface runs in, with `Auto` modes resolved as wgpu does. A tool that parses the line finds a new `present …` part, and the GPU part is now `gpu <ms> ms <note>: <passes>`.
**What to do.** Nothing. To compare passes, time them with `FLINT_VSYNC=0` or `flint render --gpu-time`.
