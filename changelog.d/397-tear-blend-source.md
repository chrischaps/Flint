### `blend_source = "tear"`: props turn to their style only where reality tears, and render mode 6, the tear alone (#397)
**What changed.** A style component (`ascii_style`, `pencil_style`, `watercolor_style`, a user style's `<name>_style`) or a `style_mask` takes `blend_source`: `"own"` (the default, as before) or `"tear"`, which multiplies the entity's `blend` by the reality tear's mask at each pixel, so the prop is styled only where render modes 1-4 tear. Render mode 6 (`set_render_mode(6, mix)`, `--render-mode 6`, F4's "Tear (props)") is a tear with no look of its own: only those props show it. With no tear on, a torn prop is at blend 0 and costs nothing. `set_style_param(id, "blend_source", "tear")` and `get_style_param` take it. `pencil_style` and `watercolor_style` now declare `blend` too, which the framework already read. The tear's noise moved to `crates/flint-render/src/tear_mask.wgsl`, shared by the composite and the style mask; renders are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. To have props tear into text, give them `blend_source = "tear"` and drive a tear from a script (`examples/ascii_tear.scene.toml`).
