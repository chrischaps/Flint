### `flint playtest --render`: screenshots and `frame` checks with no window (#1191)
**What changed.** `flint playtest --render` draws every frame into an offscreen texture through the player's own renderer and HUD, so models load, `--screenshot` / `--screenshot-at` / `--screenshot-every` write PNGs at game times, and the report gains `render` (frame size, frames drawn, draw calls) and `screenshots`. Input scripts take `screenshot <file>` (the next frame drawn, in `flint play` too) and `frame` checks: `expect frame hud contains "<text>"` / `lacks`, `frame style <name> on|off`, `frame draws [no] <entity>` (the entity or its model's nodes). Without `--render`, `frame hud` still reads the HUD's draw list and the other two fail saying no frame was drawn. A playtest's warning count now leaves out a library's warnings (the Vulkan loader's), as the load summary already did.
**What to do.** Nothing. To check what a frame shows, add `--render` and `frame` lines to a playtest.
