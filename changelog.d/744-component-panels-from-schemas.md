### A component's schema gives it an F3 panel: `panel = "status" | "tune" | "trigger"`; the Day / Time, Weather, Reality, Visitor and Dead Calm panels are gone (#744)
**What changed.** A field's schema can say what it is on its component's F3 panel: `panel = "status"` (read only, on the status line), `"tune"` (an editor that writes the live component, with Reset and Commit to File) or `"trigger"` (a button that writes once for the script to clear), or a table with `role` and `label`, `labels`, `format`, `scale`, `unset`, `from`, `min`/`max`, `step`, `slider`, `presets`, `commit`, `press`; `[component.<name>.panel] title` names it. Every entity carrying such a component gets a panel. The engine's five panels for `time_of_day`, `weather`, `reality`, `raft_visitor` and `dead_calm` are removed, with `TimeOfDayDebugPanel`, `WeatherDebugPanel`, `RealityDebugPanel`, `VisitorDebugPanel` and `DeadCalmDebugPanel`. `DebugPanel` has `sync(&mut PanelCtx)` and `weight()` (defaulted), and `assign_columns` takes weights. A mistake in a panel line is a `bad-panel` warning.
**What to do.** A game that used those panels writes `panel` lines in its schemas (the book's Debug Panels page, "A Panel From a Schema"); field-level lines load on an older engine too. A game binary calling `assign_columns` passes `panel.weight()` for each panel.
