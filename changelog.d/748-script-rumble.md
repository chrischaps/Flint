### `rumble(pad, strength, seconds)` buzzes a gamepad from any script; pads come from `flint_runtime::pad` (#748)
**What changed.** Scripts have a general `rumble(pad, strength, seconds)`: both motors of a pad (`-1` for every pad, else the index an input config's `gamepad = n` names) at 0..1 for up to 10 seconds, in gilrs's 50 ms ticks. A new call on a pad replaces what it was playing, and 0 strength or length stops it. It needs no music session (while one runs, the session's haptics own the motors and `rumble()` does nothing), and with no pad, a build without the `gamepad` feature or the browser it does nothing. The gilrs wrapper moved from `flint_input_capture::pad` to `flint_runtime::pad`, behind `flint-runtime`'s new `gamepad` feature (off by default; flint-player's `gamepad` turns it on). `flint_input_capture::pad` still re-exports it.
**What to do.** A game binary that names `flint_input_capture::pad` should name `flint_runtime::pad`; one that depends on `flint-runtime` and reads pads itself adds `features = ["gamepad"]`. Scripts: nothing.
