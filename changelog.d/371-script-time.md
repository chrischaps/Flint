### `flint render --script-time T` captures what a game's scripts make of a scene (#371)
**What changed.** `--script-time T` runs the scene's scripts to game time `T` and captures there: it is `--scripts --time T` in one flag, beside `--particle-time` and `--anim-time`, and takes what `--time` takes (`0..8 -o x.gif`, a list `2,5`) with `--frame-step` and `--fps`. Giving it with `--time` is an error. A render that doesn't run a scene's scripts now says so on stderr, naming the entities that carry them: a plain still used to say nothing. A render whose scripts ran no longer also warns "no living props, so only its other clocks move". The wording of the scripts warning in `--report json`'s `warnings` changed (it now names the flag).
**What to do.** Nothing. A check that greps a render's stderr for silence may now see the scripts warning on a scene with scripts; add `--script-time` to capture what they do, or expect the line.
