### `flint asset generate|resolve|regenerate --max-cost`, one AI cost ledger, `.flint/prices.toml`, `flint asset job resume` (#721)
**What changed.** AI asset generation now goes through the cost meter `flint gen` uses: `--max-cost <USD>` refuses a Flux, Meshy or ElevenLabs call before it is sent if it could pass the ceiling (`resolve` reports `over_budget` and exits 1 with an asset left unresolved). Every paid call, asset or procgen or look, is appended to one ledger, `.flint/ai_costs.jsonl` at the project root; the old `.flint/procgen_costs.json` is still read, never written. `flint asset costs` and `flint gen --cost-report` print the same report, whose text heading is now "By spec or asset". Prices are data: a project's `.flint/prices.toml` overrides any built-in rate. A Meshy model is a job from submit (`flint asset job list`), and `flint asset job resume <id>` finishes one a run didn't. In Rust, `GenerationProvider::submit_job`/`poll_job`/`download_result` and `JobPollResult` are gone, and `create_provider` and `resolve_scene` take a `Calls`.
**What to do.** Set your Meshy plan's credit price in `.flint/prices.toml` (`[calls.meshy] unit = "task"`, `usd = ...`). Point any script reading `procgen_costs.json` at `ai_costs.jsonl`.
