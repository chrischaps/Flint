### The style registry is a `StyleRegistry` a renderer or host owns (#742)
**What changed.** Styles are no longer registered for the process (ADR 0129). `stylize::register(&STYLE)` is `styles.register(&STYLE)` on a `StyleRegistry`; `stylize::find`, `registry()` and `names()` are its methods (`builtin(name)` / `builtins()` for the engine's own); `custom::load_dir`, `load_for`, `load_file`, `reload_changed_for`, `errors` and `source` are too, and `custom::drop_unseen` is gone (a scene's registry holds only the user styles it sees: `StyleRegistry::for_scene`). Readers take the registry: `post_process_config_from_def(def, &styles)`, `StyleSettings::from_tables`, `StyleSpec::parse`, `apply_style_spec(s)`, `post_style::*`, `entity_style::*`, `LookFade::new`, `paint_style_errors`. `SceneBinding` makes the scene's (`SceneBinding::over(scope, &base)` keeps a host's game styles); `bind_scene` hands it to the renderer (`styles()`, `styles_mut()`, `set_styles`). A user style edited while running is freed once rebuilt. A look naming a style the engine lacks is judged by the scene it is laid on.
**What to do.** A game with `PlayerExtension::styles`: nothing. One that called `stylize::register` itself: register into the registry it reads scenes with and hand that to its renderer (`app.core.styles` in an embedded player).
