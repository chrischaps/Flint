### The player keeps its last 600 frames and judges each for a hitch; `FLINT_HITCH_FACTOR`, `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS`, `GameClock::paced_delta_time` (#1076)
**What changed.** Every `flint-player` and `flint play` run, and every game binary embedding `PlayerApp`, now records each frame whether or not `--perf` is on. A record holds the frame's phases, the clock's raw and paced time, the present-to-present time, the physics steps that ran and the GPU time. The last 600 frames are kept, and each one is judged for a hitch: a frame over both twice the rolling median and the median plus 8 ms. Under vsync pacing the paced time is judged, so Fifo's 6/6/6/45 ms bursts don't count. Recording costs about 0.65 µs a frame in an optimized build and allocates nothing per frame once the ring is full. Nothing shows the result yet: the F2 graph and the hitch log are #1078 and #1079. `--perf`'s line and `?perf=1`'s readout print what they did. `GameClock::paced_delta_time()` is new: the last tick's wall time in whole refresh periods, or `None` when the clock isn't pacing. `GpuTimer::take_latest_frame_ms()` and `SceneRenderer::take_gpu_frame_ms()` give the newest read-back frame's GPU time, unsmoothed.
**What to do.** Nothing. To change what counts as a hitch, set `FLINT_HITCH_FACTOR` (at least 1, default 2) or `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS` (default 8).
