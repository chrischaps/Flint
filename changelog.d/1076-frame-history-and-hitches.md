### The player keeps its last 600 frames and judges each for a hitch; the `frame-history` feature, `FLINT_FRAME_HISTORY`, `FLINT_HITCH_FACTOR`, `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS` (#1076)
**What changed.** With flint-player's new `frame-history` feature (on by default), the player records every frame, `--perf` or not. A record holds the phases, the clock's raw and paced time, present-to-present, the physics steps and the GPU time. Each frame is judged a hitch when it is over both 2x the rolling median and the median + 8 ms, on the paced clock under vsync. It costs about 0.65 µs a frame. Nothing shows it yet (#1078, #1079), and `--perf`'s output is unchanged. Without the feature, frames are recorded only under `--perf`, as before. New API: `GameClock::paced_delta_time()` and `SceneRenderer::take_gpu_frame_ms()`.
**What to do.** Nothing, to keep it. A shipped game drops it by building flint-player with `default-features = false` and the features it uses. `FLINT_FRAME_HISTORY=0|1` overrides the build at run time. `FLINT_HITCH_FACTOR` (default 2) and `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS` (default 8) set the thresholds.
