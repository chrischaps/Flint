### `flint render --frame-dump <file.json>`: the frame's passes, draws, targets and timings as data (#1084)
**What changed.** `flint render` can write the frame it drew as JSON: every pass in the order recorded (named as `--gpu-time`'s rows), whether it ran and why not, the targets it read and wrote, its GPU time in that frame, and each draw by entity name, with the culled ones and their reason (frustum, lod, draw distance). Then the frame's settings, targets, lights and `RenderStats`. A `--time` span describes its last frame, and `--report json` gains `frame_dump`. In Rust, `SceneRenderer::describe_next_frame` / `describe_frame` and `flint_render::frame_capture::FrameDescription` (`"format": "flintframe"`, `"version": 1`) do the same for any host, and `RenderStats` is now `serde`. A frame nobody describes draws as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. To ask why a scene makes the draw calls it does, `flint render <scene> --frame-dump frame.json` and read the `Scene` pass's `draws`.
