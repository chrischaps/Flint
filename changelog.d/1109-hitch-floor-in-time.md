### A hitch is now at least 24 ms past the median, at any refresh rate: `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS` defaults to 24 (#1109)
**What changed.** The hitch detector's floor went from 8 ms to 24 ms. A frame must still be over twice the median of the frames before it as well. On a 60 Hz display nothing changes, because the shortest hitch is still three refreshes (50 ms). On faster displays the floor no longer lets two missed refreshes through. At 240 Hz a frame that stood three refreshes (12.5 ms against 4.2) was a hitch and is not now; the shortest is seven (29.2 ms). The F2 graph's count, the hitch log, `--perf`'s line, `?perf=1` and the `--max-hitches` gate all read the same rule, so on a high-refresh machine they count fewer hitches. Without vsync, a 60 fps game's shortest hitch goes from 34 ms to 41 ms.
**What to do.** Nothing. To keep the old count for a comparison, set `FLINT_HITCH_FLOOR_MS=8`.
