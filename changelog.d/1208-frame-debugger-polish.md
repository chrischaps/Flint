### `flint frame diff` counts draw kinds; render captures name their engine commit (#1208)
**What changed.** `flint frame diff`'s "Draws by entity" lines count an entity's draw kinds instead of listing every draw: `removed  body in Shadow cascade 0: 91 draws: skinned shadow`, or `3 draws (2 pbr, 1 unlit)` when there are several (was `1 draw (pbr)`, and a 91-part body's kind 91 times). The `--json` document is unchanged. `flint render --frame-capture` now writes `engine.commit` and `engine.dirty` when the scene's project is in a git checkout, as the player's captures do, and `flint frame replay` says whether it replays at the commit that captured the frame or across commits. In `flint frame open`, a replay of a player capture keeps the captured frame's CPU phases, scripts and history in the bottom strip, marked "as captured", instead of saying the capture came from `flint render`.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a script of yours reads `frame diff`'s text: read its `--json` instead.
