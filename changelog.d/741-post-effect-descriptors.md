### `[post_process]` keys are listed, range-checked and declared once; `flint render --list-effects` (#741)
**What changed.** Every built-in effect key (`exposure`, `bloom_*`, `ssao_*`, `fog_*`, `grade_*`, ...) is declared once in `flint_schema::post_effects`, with a default, the range its effect is tuned for and help, and the scene parse, `PostProcessConfig`, the schema, `--sweep post.<key>` and the F4 sections are made from it. `flint render --list-effects [--json]` lists them. A scene value past its range draws as before and warns (`post-range`, in the load, `flint validate` and `flint check`); in a look or a `--sweep post.<key>` it is refused, as a style value is. `bloom_soft_threshold` and `ssao_bias` are new scene keys (same defaults). `engine/schemas/scene/post_process.toml` no longer lists the effect keys: the registry adds them. `PostProcessConfig::fxaa_enabled` is now `fxaa`, the scene key's name.
**What to do.** Rust that sets `config.fxaa_enabled` sets `config.fxaa`. A game layer's own `post_process.toml` may drop its copies of the effect keys. Run `flint check` and settle any `post-range` warning.
