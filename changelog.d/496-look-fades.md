### Looks cross-fade: `set_look(name, seconds)`, `clear_look(seconds)`, `look_faded` (#496)
**What changed.** `set_look` and `clear_look` take an optional length in seconds of game time and ease from the look drawn now to the new one: numbers and colours lerp, an effect one look turns on ramps its strength from 0, a style only one look has fades by its whole-frame `blend`, and options (a ramp, a palette) switch halfway. Only what the two looks differ in moves, and scripts' per-frame post overrides still win. On arrival every script's `on_event` hears `look_faded` with `#{ look }`. F4's Look picker has a Fade time; `flint render --scripts --time T` captures a fade at T. The one-argument forms are the cut they always were. For Rust hosts: `ScriptCommand::SetLook` has a `seconds` field, `ScriptHost::set_look` takes `(name, seconds)`, `HeadlessGame::take_look_request` returns a `LookRequest`, and `PlayerApp::fade_to_look(look, seconds)` is new.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game binary that implements `ScriptHost::set_look` or matches on `SetLook` adds the `seconds` field. See `examples/look_fade.scene.toml`.
