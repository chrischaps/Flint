### Pencil `tone = "light"`: hatch the shadows, not the dark paint (#869)
**What changed.** The pencil style takes a new option, `tone = "picture" | "light"`, in `[post_process.styles.pencil]`, on `pencil_style` entities and as `--style pencil:tone=light`. `picture`, the default, is the old behaviour: hatching follows the picture's luminance, so a dark material is hatched like a shadow. `light` divides the picture by each opaque mesh's own colour, so a brown floor in the sun stays blank and the cast shadows are hatched. One `brightness` (0 on the benches) then holds across scenes. While a row asks for it, the renderer draws a new "style albedo" pass after the scene, about 0.03 ms at 1080p. Terrain, grass, water, particles and shallow-wooze draws aren't in it, and keep the picture's tone. A Rust style can read the albedo with `.uses_albedo(..)` and `StyleFrame::albedo`. Existing renders and the `rotoscope` look are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. To shade by light, set `tone = "light"`. A `brightness` raised to lift dark paint can come back toward 0: `rotoscope`'s 0.5 still hatches the shadows under `light`, only lighter.
