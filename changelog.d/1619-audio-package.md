### Audio is a package a game can leave out; music and rhythm require it (#1619)
**Package.** audio
**What changed.** Sound is the `audio` package (`packages/audio/`), a standard one: every game has it unless its `flint.toml` writes `audio = false` under `[packages]`, and then `flint build` makes a player with no flint-audio, no AudioWorklet mixer, no kira and no symphonia. `music` and `rhythm` now `[require]` it, so leaving audio out leaves them out too, and `flint check` names the requirement. With the package in:
- `flint-audio` and `flint-audio-worklet` moved to `packages/audio/crates/`, and the `audio_source` and `audio_trigger` schemas to `packages/audio/schemas/`.
- The `audio` script module is the package's, with the same functions. It is enabled in every scene, as before. The `music` module (`music_beat()`, `music_bar()`, `music_section()`) is the music package's, also enabled in every scene, and still reads -1 with no music playing. A build without music has no such functions, where it used to answer -1.
- The `audio` game-state policy is the package's. A game state may still name it in any build.
- The sound card's build option is `sound` now (it was `audio`, a name the package took): `--features sound` on flint-player and flint-cli. A build with the package and without `sound` plays silent, as a headless CLI does.
- `--music-volume` and `--sfx-volume` are the package's player flags, and work as before.
- The frame has a new `Stage::Sound`, after `ScriptUi`, where the package plays the frame's triggers. `StageCtx::events` lends the frame's game events, and `SceneCtx::preload_audio` the scene's `[scene] preload_audio`.
- A host asks the sound what it needs through `flint_package::sound` (the `Sound` trait and the `SoundPoint` registration): open it, start it from the web's Begin gesture, hold it, read its buses and meters. `PlayerApp::audio` is gone.
**What to do.** A game's Rust extension that used `ctx.audio` calls `flint_audio::player::audio(ctx.resources)` (an `Option<ExtensionAudio>`) and depends on `flint-audio` with `features = ["player"]`. `flint_player::{Bus, Buses, ExtensionAudio, SoundOptions}` are `flint_audio::{Bus, Buses}` and `flint_audio::player::{ExtensionAudio, SoundOptions}`. A cargo command that turned on `--features audio` for the sound card asks for `sound`. Scenes, scripts and `flint.toml` need nothing.
