### `[living] pose = "noise"`: space-colonization and grammar trees can live (#506)
**What changed.** A `tree_v1` spec's `[living]` table takes `pose = "noise"` and a `[living.noise]` table (`turn` in degrees, default 12; `wavelength` in metres, default 3; `stretch`, default 0.08). Each pose keeps the tree's own branches and bends them on smooth noise instead of regrowing them, so `branch_method = "space_colonization"` and text grammars (`lsystem_axiom`/`lsystem_rules`, `lsystem_preset`) now live, where `check_living` used to refuse them. On a colonized tree, `depth` in the living skeleton is now its branch order, which keeps per-level stagger holding on the beat. A tree with more segments than bones now folds a step off every twig in turn, not a whole twig at once. Trees that fold only a few tips fold as before. `form_v1` refuses `pose = "noise"`. A spec without the key bakes and renders as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. To bring a pine or willow to life, add `pose = "noise"` under its `[living]` (`examples/specs/grove_pine.procgen.toml`).
