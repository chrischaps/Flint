### `flint edit` holds motion on twos: `--motion-hold`, and the previewer's Hold switch (#871)
**What changed.** The editors draw `motion_hold` now; until this change they ignored it. The model previewer (`flint edit model.glb`) holds the pose on ticks of the clip's own time, the one its timeline shows, so playing and scrubbing step through the same drawings. Turn it on with the Animation panel's **Hold** switch and rate, or with `--motion-hold 12`. A layer dial, a posed bone or a clip switch shows at once. The scene viewer (`flint edit scene.toml`) holds what a scene's `motion_hold` components and `[post_process] motion_hold` hold, on its own wall clock, and `--motion-hold` writes over the scene's rate. The viewer plays no animation and runs no physics, so there only a gizmo drag of a held entity steps. `SceneRenderer::release_motion_hold(entity)` lets one hand-made pose through a hold.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene with no hold looks and costs the same in both editors.
