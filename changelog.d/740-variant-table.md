### A crowd of per-entity props keeps every prop's style, and a cap names what it leaves out (#740)
**What changed.** Pencil, watercolor and user styles with `inputs = ["mask"]` held 63, 15 and 15 per-entity variants a frame, and they and the ownership pass (#384) stopped at mask id 255: past that, props drew plain or were drawn over by a whole-frame style, mostly with no message. They now share ASCII's growing variant table, so every variant gets a row and every id an owner (300 props on `grid = "object"` all draw). A user style's prelude declares `flint_params` as a storage buffer (`flint_params.rows[i]` still reads row `i`) and the mask id slots as `flint_slots` at group 0 binding 2. A cap that still drops something (ASCII's 8 dissolve bands, 8 snap grids and 8 fonts, one paper image a frame) warns once naming the first entity past it, and `flint render --report json` lists them as `style_drops`.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a user style's WGSL read `flint_params.slots` directly: call `flint_slot(id)` instead.
