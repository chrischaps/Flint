### `flint gen`'s AI and cost flags are the ai package's own (#1592)
**Package.** ai
**What changed.** `--ai-create`, `--ai-assist`, `--ai-refine`, `--reference`, `--ai-generator`, `--ai-provider`, `--ai-model`, `--ai-iterations`, `--refine-rounds`, `--max-cost`, `--cost-report`, `--since` and `--style-guide` are now flags the ai package adds to `gen`, as `render --ai-refine` has been since #1572. A stock flint's `flint gen --help` is unchanged, byte for byte. A flint built without the ai package no longer lists them, and given one it exits 2 naming the package; before, it listed them, refused five of them by name and ignored the rest (`--ai-provider`, `--max-cost` and the like did nothing). In such a build `--print-spec` and `--save-spec`, which are procgen's, are listed with the other options instead of under "AI assistance". `flint gen <set>.glyphs.toml` refuses every one of the ai package's flags by name, where it ignored `--cost-report`, `--ai-provider` and the like.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a build leaves out the ai package and a script passes one of these flags: drop the flag, or list `ai` under `[packages]`.
