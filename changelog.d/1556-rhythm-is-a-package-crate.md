### The rhythm crates live in `engine/packages/rhythm/crates/` (#1556)
**What changed.** flint-rhythm and flint-input-capture moved into the rhythm package's folder, beside a new crate, flint-rhythm-game, which holds what flint-player and flint-cli knew of the rhythm game: the charted session's subsystem (`flint_rhythm_game::player::PACKAGE`, behind its `player` feature, with the Music Guide and Manifest Map panels behind `debug-hud`) and `flint music calibrate` and `flint music chart play|replay` (`flint_rhythm_game::cli`, behind `cli`). The rhythm manifest names it in `[rust]`, so both launchers list it from there. Every command, flag, judgment and log is as before: a replayed session judges byte for byte as it did. `flint_package::SceneCtx` has a new `post` field, the scene's `[post_process]` as the host read it.
**What to do.** A game whose own `Cargo.toml` names `engine/crates/flint-rhythm` or `engine/crates/flint-input-capture` by path changes it to `engine/packages/rhythm/crates/flint-rhythm` (or `flint-input-capture`). A game that builds a `SceneCtx` by hand adds `post: None` (or its scene's `post_process.as_ref()`). Nothing else.
