### A camera rig for scripts: `set_camera_on_path`, `shake_view`, `set_hand_held`, `shot`, `noise` (#1158)
**What changed.** The new `camera_rig` module flies the 3D camera along a smooth path through a list of points (`camera_path`, `camera_path_length`, `set_camera_on_path` with `look_at`, `look_ahead`, `bank`, `max_bank` and `closed`), at an even speed by distance and leaning into its turns from the path's own curvature. `shake_view(amplitude, frequency, decay)` shakes it across its own view (right and up, plus a small turn) from seeded noise, laid over whatever camera the scripts set this frame; `set_hand_held(strength, #{ steps })` adds a hand-held drift and a walk's bob; `shot(t, starts)` says which shot of a list a time is in, how far through, and whether it began this frame. `noise(x)` / `noise(x, seed)` is smooth seeded noise in the math module. All run on game time, so a `--frame-step` capture makes the same frames every run. `shake_camera` and the `camera_2d` module are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that draws its own Catmull-Rom, sums sines for a shake or chains `if t < ...` for its cuts can call these instead ([Scripting → Camera Rig API](docs/book/src/concepts/scripting.md#camera-rig-api)). A script that defines its own `fn noise(x)` keeps it: a script's function wins over the engine's.
