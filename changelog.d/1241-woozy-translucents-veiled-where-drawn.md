### A `shallow_wooze` translucent is veiled and fogged where it is drawn, not at its rest pose (#1241)
**What changed.** A blended mesh with `shallow_wooze` (a drifting pane of glass, a woozy soft-edged body) is now redrawn through its woozy vertex stage in the style veil and the fog veil, as the main pass draws it. In front of a styled entity it unmasks the style where the pane is drawn (it unmasked where the pane had been, so glyphs showed through one side of the glass and a dimmed ghost stood on the other), and in fog it is fogged at its own depth (it was fogged as what lay behind it, beside a fogless ghost of itself). A woozy body translucent only at its soft edge, which the main pass blends whole, is now fogged at its own depth too. A scene with no woozy translucent draws as before, byte for byte.
**What to do.** Nothing.
