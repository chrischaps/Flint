### `flint_living::ResolvedProp` is read by knob: `prop.get(LivingKnob::Softness)` (#1025)
**What changed.** Every Living Props knob is one row of `flint_procgen::living_knob_rows!`, and the enum it makes, `flint_procgen::LivingKnob`, is the one the panel (`flint_debug_ui::Knob`, now a re-export), the runtime and the player all use. `ResolvedProp` lost its field per knob: `prop.get(knob)` returns that knob's `Resolved<KnobValue>` and `prop.iter()` every one. `KnobValue` is `Number`, `Count`, `Flag` or `Name` (a stagger by name) and compares with a plain `f32`, `u32`, `bool` or `Stagger`. The scene-table readers that were `flint_player`'s (`defaults_of`, `species_of`, `species_problems`, `apply_preset` and the per-knob checks) are `flint_living`'s now; `flint_player::apply_living_preset` and `flint_player::species_problems` still work. `clear_living`'s error lists its keys in the panel's order. `flint render --report json` is unchanged, key for key and byte for byte.
**What to do.** A game binary that read `resolved.softness.value` writes `resolved.get(LivingKnob::Softness).value`; one that called `Knob::desc()` or `Knob::in_section` imports `flint_debug_ui::KnobPanel`. Scenes, presets and scripts need nothing.
