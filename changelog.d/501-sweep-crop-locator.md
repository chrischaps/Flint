### `--sweep-crop` sheets show where the crop sits, and take fractions (#501)
**What changed.** A `flint render --sweep ... --sweep-crop` sheet now carries a locator in its header: the first cell's whole frame, shrunk, with the kept region outlined and the rest dimmed, so a reader sees which part of the frame every cell comes from. The crop can also be given as fractions of the frame, `--sweep-crop 0.4,0.48,0.33,0.33`, so one crop works at any `--width`/`--height`; a value with a decimal point makes the whole crop fractional, and each value must then be between 0 and 1. Pixel crops (`700,380,480,270`) mean what they did. A crop sheet is taller by the locator where its header was shorter than it, so a golden of a crop sweep needs re-blessing.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a game keeps a golden of a `--sweep-crop` sheet: re-bless it.
