### `flint goldens` sequence cases hold shimmer to a budget; `flint render --last-frame` (#478)
**What changed.** A `[[case]]` in a `*.goldens.toml` may give `frames` (with `orbit` or `camera_path`, and `frame_dt`) and `max_reversals` (optionally `max_flips`). Such a case has no golden: it draws the sequence on one renderer, counts the ASCII style's glyph flips and A-B-A reversals from its own grid, and fails when the mean a frame passes its budget. Its `report.json` entry gains a `sequence` object, and the contact sheet charts every frame. A sequence case refuses golden keys and sequence flags in `args`, and a sequence key without `frames` is refused. `flint render --frames N --last-frame` writes only the last frame, to `--output` itself. Existing manifests and renders are unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. To guard a temporal style in your own suite, add a sequence case with a budget measured well under what a broken history reads.
