### A collider's `layer`, `mask` and scale changed by a script are heard; `set_collision_layer`, `set_collision_mask` (#1221)
**What changed.** A collider's `layer` and `mask` and its entity's world scale used to be read once, when its body was made. Now a change is heard at the next physics step: `set_field(id, "collider", "layer", "ghost")` puts it on the new layer (a body at rest is woken), and `set_scale` on the entity or a parent remakes its collider at the new size, `offset` included. New script functions `set_collision_layer(id, layer)` and `set_collision_mask(id, mask)` write the same fields and refuse a name that is no layer at the call. A step where nothing was written costs less than before (26 us against 81 us for 2000 colliders): `FlintWorld::write_stamp(id)` is new, and a collider whose entity and ancestors weren't written isn't read.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a game changed `transform.scale` on a physics entity and relied on its collider keeping its old size: it now follows the drawn size, as one made at that scale would. `PhysicsSync::update_sensor_flags` is now `update_colliders`, and `sync_from_rapier` takes `&mut self`.
