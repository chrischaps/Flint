### A point light casts a shadow with `cast_shadows = true` (#1112)
**What changed.** `cast_shadows = true` on a point light draws a cube of six shadow maps around it, out to its `range`, softened by its `source_radius` as a spot's is. A lantern on a table shadows the floor around the table on every side. One point light casts, the first by entity name that asks; another that asks lights without one and logs `shadow-cap` naming the one that does. That's a budget of its own, so a spot and a point light can both cast. A point light asking no longer logs "a point light casts no shadow yet". Meshes, skinned meshes, terrain, grass and the ocean take the shadow. The shadow map texture holds one more layer, 16 MB at the default 2048 texels, whose six faces are each a third of `shadow_resolution` wide. `point_light_at` in `local_lights.wgsl` takes the receiver's normal and pixel now, as `spot_light_at` does. A scene that doesn't ask renders as it did.
**What to do.** Nothing. To shadow from a lamp, add `cast_shadows = true` to its point light, and `material.cast_shadows = false` to the lamp's own glass or shade, which would otherwise shadow everything around it.
