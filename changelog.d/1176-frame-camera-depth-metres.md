### A frame capture names its camera, and `flint frame image` shows depth in metres (#1176)
**What changed.** A capture's `manifest.json` has a `camera` block: the camera the frame drew with (position, target, fov, near, far, orthographic and its height), and a comic panel's under its panel. `flint frame image <capture> Scene --target depth` and the frame debugger's `Auto` view turn the scene's depth into metres through it, on a log scale from the nearest drawn (white) to the farthest (dark), and the `view` line names the range (`depth 2.67..86.0 m, log scale, near white`). The debugger has a `Metres` mode beside `Depth`. `flint frame show` prints a `Camera` line. `replay/inputs.json` no longer carries its own camera: the replay reads the manifest's. A capture written before this has no `camera`: its depth keeps the old stretch over the stored values, and its replay reads the camera from `inputs.json` as before. Shadow map layers keep the stretch too.
**What to do.** Nothing.
