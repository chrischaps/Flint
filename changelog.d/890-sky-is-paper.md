### Pencil `tone = "light"` leaves the sky as paper (#890)
**What changed.** Under `[post_process.styles.pencil] tone = "light"` (or `pencil_style { tone = "light" }`), a pixel where nothing was drawn (the sky, depth cleared to 1) now has tone 1, blank paper, whatever the sky's colour and whatever `brightness` and `contrast` say. Before, the sky kept the picture's own luminance, so a dark sky was hatched all over; the `rotoscope` look, which takes its tone from the light, did that on any scene with a dark sky. Terrain, grass and water are drawn surfaces and keep the picture's luminance as before. `tone = "picture"` is unchanged and still hatches a dark sky.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a look wanted a hatched sky under `tone = "light"`: switch it to `tone = "picture"`.
