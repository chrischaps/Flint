### Living props' runtime is the `flint-living` crate; the resolver lends it a budget (#1024)
**What changed.** `LivingProps` and everything that runs living props over time (`LivingPropRequest`, `LivingPropId`, `LineageDone`, `LivingStats`, `BlendFrame`, `PropBlend`, `LivingDefaults`, `LivingLayer(s)`, `LivingScope`, `LivingShadows`, `Resolved`, `ResolvedProp`, the `*Override`s, `jitter_rate_clamped`, `row_rate_clamped`, `POSE_CACHE_BYTES`) moved from `flint_procgen` to `flint_living`, and `ProcGenResolver` no longer holds the props. Its living methods are `LivingProps` methods that take the resolver: `resolver.add_living_prop(r)` is `props.add_prop(&mut resolver, r)`, `make_living_now()` is `props.make_now(&resolver)`, `process_living(dt)` / `process_living_in(dt, w)` are `props.process(&resolver, dt, w)`, `step_living_now` is `step_now`, `process_living_beside(side, ..)` is `props.process_beside(side, &resolver, ..)`, `settle_living_now(side)` is `props.settle(&resolver, side)`, `set_living_defaults` is `set_defaults`, `clear_living_props` is `clear_props`, and `living_props()` is the props themselves. The resolver gained `living_spec(name)`, `budget_left_ms()` and `set_frame_budget_ms(ms)`. `JITTER_RATE_MAX`, `ROW_RATE_MAX`, `LivingGenerator`, `LivingRig` and the `[living]` / `[wooze]` tables stay in `flint_procgen`. A scene, spec or script sees no difference: frames are byte-identical.
**What to do.** Nothing for a scene or script. A game binary that named one of the moved types adds `flint-living` beside `flint-procgen` and changes the path; `flint-player` builds without `living` no longer link the scheduler.
