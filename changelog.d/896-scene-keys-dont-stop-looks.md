### A scene's unknown `[post_process]` key no longer stops `set_look` and F4's Look (#896)
**What changed.** A scene whose `[post_process]` held a key that isn't a field (Float's scenes had `enabled = true` and `vignette = 0.18`) loaded with a `warning[unknown-field]`, then failed every look laid under its keys: the scene's own `look = "..."` (in `flint render` and the player alike), `set_look`, F4's Look picker, comic panels and `flint render --scripts` all printed `[post_process] has no field 'enabled'` and drew the frame without the look. When a look is laid under the scene's keys, the scene's keys now go on as the loader read them: an unknown key is left out, and a value past its tuned range (which only warns at load) is drawn as written. The load-time warning is still the only message about such a key, so `flint check` no longer calls such a scene's own look a `bad-look` error. A look file's own mistakes are refused as before, naming the look.
**What to do.** Nothing. To quiet the warning, remove the key (`flint check` names it); `vignette = x` is `vignette_enabled = true` with `vignette_intensity = x`.
