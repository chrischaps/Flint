### The swapchain acquire is its own phase, `acquire`, and counts as `elsewhere` (#1123)
**What changed.** The player used to time its wait for the next swapchain image as part of the `render` phase. A compositor stall, or a full swapchain waiting for the GPU, therefore read as the engine's own render work. That wait is now its own phase, `acquire`. Like `present`, it is outside the player's work, so a hitch whose excess is in it is classed `elsewhere`, and its cause line reads `elsewhere, acquire 515.6 ms`. `--perf`'s line, `?perf=1`'s readout and a frame capture's `cpu` records list `acquire` beside the other phases. The F2 graph draws it in the grey `elsewhere` band. Each `--hitch-log` line gains `acquire_ms` (beside `present_ms`, filled for `elsewhere` hitches) and `gpu_ms`, the GPU time read back in that frame (`null` while the GPU isn't timed). `render` now reads lower wherever the wait was large: under vsync, `FLINT_VSYNC=0`, or a GPU-heavy look.
**What to do.** Nothing. A tool that reads the hitch log by key sees two new keys, and one that compares `render` across engine versions should add `acquire` back in.
