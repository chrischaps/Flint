### Terrain, skinned meshes and the ocean are lit like every other mesh (#738)
**What changed.** The lighting WGSL is written once (`lights.wgsl`, `brdf.wgsl`, `shadows.wgsl`, `direct_light.wgsl`, `tonemap.wgsl`, `pbr_surface.wgsl` in `crates/flint-render/src/`), not copied into six shaders, so three surfaces that had drifted now match the meshes. Skinned meshes take `[environment]` `diffuse_wrap`, `oren_nayar` and sheen, and a light's `source_radius`. Terrain takes those too, plus the normal offset on the sun's shadow (less acne on slopes) and the meshes' specular. The ocean's shadow softens under a sun with an `angular_size`, as other surfaces' shadows do. A scene that sets none of these levers renders as before, apart from terrain under a casting sun. Here that moved two stylize goldens (both terrain), the skinned characters in `phase4_runtime`, and the terrain in `rolling_meadow`.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a scene's skinned characters or terrain were tuned to make up for the missing levers: look at them again. Rust that built a pipeline from `include_str!("shader.wgsl")` (none outside the engine) uses `wgsl_sources::SCENE` now.
