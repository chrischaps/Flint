### `tune_living(knobs, seconds)` eases Living Props knobs instead of stepping them (#514)
**What changed.** `tune_living` takes an optional last argument of seconds, as `set_wooze` does. With it, the continuous knobs (`step`, `glide`, `amplitude`, `jitter`, `echoes.spread`, `echoes.alpha`, `variance.amount`, `variance.steady`, `softness`) glide from where they stand to the target over that time on the field's smoothstep, for the whole grove or one species (`species: name`). The discrete ones (`echoes.count`, `stagger`, `variance.keep_size`, the boil) switch at the start. The grove's clock knobs ease on the wooze field's own ramps; the rest on the living clock, which a freeze or a paused state stops. A ramp started on a knob that's already easing starts from where it stands, and asking every frame for the same target still arrives on time. A knob set at once (no seconds, the F3 panel, a preset, `clear_living`) takes over from its ramp. `tune_living(map)` with no seconds is unchanged. In flint-core, `field::Ramp` is public, and `ParamField::global_target` / `WoozeField::global_target` say where the global knobs are heading.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that eases a knob itself, calling `tune_living` every frame, can pass seconds once instead.
