### `flint profile` and the profiler tab read a trace one way, and Compare rings the scope `explain` names (#1287)
**What changed.** `flint profile` and `flint frame open`'s profiler tab now share one trace reader, so they agree on a trace's frames, its parents, where a wrapped ring's scopes begin and which frame `hitch` means. The tab refuses what `flint profile` refused: a trace newer than this flint reads, a file whose `format` isn't `flint-trace`, and a file with no scopes. Compare now holds each bar to its median over the trace's normal frames (the ones `flint profile explain` uses, so hitches and the look-back's cut edges no longer count in it), splits a path by its file detail as `explain` does, and rings in gold the bar that carries the frame's excess, which is the scope `explain` names; the selection line says `carried by <scope>`. The coverage line both say ends `so the N frames before them have no scopes` (it said `are left out` in `flint profile` and `have none` in the tab). A parent scope that closed in a later frame than its children, such as a worker's long load, is now their parent in the tab too. Every `flint profile` view prints the same as before on a trace that didn't wrap.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that matched `are left out` in `flint profile`'s coverage line should match `have no scopes`.
