### `flint render --scripts --time` draws a look fade as the player does (#1157)
**What changed.** A capture inside a `set_look(name, seconds)` fade drew the fade from the look the scene file authors when an earlier `set_look` had changed it: it kept only the last look a script asked for. It now lays every look asked since the last capture in order, each fade from the frame at the moment it was asked, so a capture at T has the post values and style blends the player draws at T. In the player a fade is now timed from the game time it was asked: the frame of the request draws its start (it was one frame further on) and it arrives at exactly the asked time plus its seconds.
**What to do.** Nothing. A capture of a scene whose script cuts to one look and later fades to another draws differently mid-fade, which is the fix; re-bless a golden or still taken there.
