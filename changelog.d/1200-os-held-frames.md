### The player says when the OS held its frames back, so the hitch tools found nothing (#1200)
**What changed.** Under vsync, Windows throttles the presents of a window it doesn't show: one behind others, or a background job's opened with `--no-activate`. Every frame then stands 250 to 580 ms while the game's own work is a few ms, and a 100 ms stall can't stand out against that median, so `--capture-on-hitch`, `--hitch-log` and `--max-hitches` found nothing and didn't say why. Now a run whose frames after the first 2 s were mostly held by the OS (at least 100 ms and four refreshes each, under a quarter of it the player's work, under half the GPU's) says so once on the console as it happens, `[perf] frames held by the OS at ~412 ms (the player's work 3.1 ms): ...; FLINT_VSYNC=off measures real frame times`, and `--exit-after`'s report adds `[perf] frames held by the OS: N of M after t=2 s, ...` under its hitch count. A run whose window shows says nothing new.
**What to do.** Nothing. To look for hitches in a run whose window won't show, set `FLINT_VSYNC=off` (or pass `--vsync off`).
