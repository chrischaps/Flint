### `flint_render::wait_for_gpu` and `FLINT_GPU_WAIT_TIMEOUT`; `SurfaceError::OutOfMemory` is gone (#948)
**What changed.** wgpu 23 gave up on every GPU wait after 60 s; wgpu 30 waits as long as the GPU takes. Flint's waits now go through `flint_render::wait_for_gpu(&device)`, which has no limit unless `FLINT_GPU_WAIT_TIMEOUT=<seconds>` is set. A wait that reaches the limit panics and names the variable, so a hung GPU fails a CI run quickly instead of running into the job's time limit. `flint_render::SurfaceError` loses `OutOfMemory`, which wgpu 30 never reports. flint-render's GPU tests share their device side by side again (`FLINT_GPU_TEST_PARALLEL=0` puts them back in turn).
**What to do.** Nothing for scenes, scripts and styles. In your own Rust, remove any `SurfaceError::OutOfMemory` match arm, and replace `device.poll(wgpu::PollType::wait_indefinitely())` with `flint_render::wait_for_gpu(&device)` if you want the limit. Set `FLINT_GPU_WAIT_TIMEOUT` in CI jobs that render.
