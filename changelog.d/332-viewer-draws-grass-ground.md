### `flint edit` draws a scene's `grass_ground`; its loader is `flint_render::load_scene_grass_ground` (#332)
**What changed.** The scene viewer now grows a `grass_ground` field on open and again on each hot reload (`--watch`), as `flint play` and `flint render` do, so a meadow is no longer bare in the editor. Editing the component's `grass.*` keys or switching it off with `"grass.enabled" = false` shows on the next reload. The loader moved from flint-player into flint-render, so the viewer can share it: `flint_render::load_scene_grass_ground(world, scene_path, ..)` and `load_grass_ground_in(world, project, scene_dir, ..)` return the `GrassConfig` and entity name they set (`LoadedGrassGround`). `flint_player::load_scene_grass_ground` keeps its signature and its `bool` and calls the new one.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game binary that loads a `grass_ground` itself can call the flint-render function instead and drop its flint-player dependency there.
