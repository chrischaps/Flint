### A held entity's lights and emitters step with it (`motion_hold`) (#873)
**What changed.** Under `motion_hold` (a component on the entity or an ancestor, or `[post_process] motion_hold` / `--motion-hold`), a point or spot light the held entity carries now shines from where the entity was drawn when its tick began, so its pool of light steps with the drawing instead of gliding. A `particle_emitter` or `particle_effect` on a held entity emits from the held drawing: particles are born only there, not along the jump to the next drawing, so a moving emitter's trail comes out as beads. Emission over distance still counts the whole way, `inherit_velocity` still sees the smooth motion, and live particles still move smoothly. Directional lights are unchanged. A game binary that steps its own `ParticleSync` holds emitters only once it calls `ParticleSync::set_motion_time(t)` (and `set_motion_hold(rate)` for a whole-frame rate) with the clock it gives `SceneRenderer::set_motion_time`; `flint-player` does both. `flint_render::motion_hold::{tick, held_rates, ...}` are re-exports of `flint_ecs::motion_hold` now.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless a scene wanted a held character's torch to glide: put `motion_hold { rate = 0 }` on the light or emitter entity.
