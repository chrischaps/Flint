### The perf line's `mean N ms a frame`, marked `GPU-bound` when the GPU holds the frames (#1318)
**What changed.** `--perf`'s line, `?perf=1`'s panel and its copied line carry a `mean` part after the frame times: the window's span over its frames, which doesn't snap to whole refreshes as one frame's time does. When the frames are unpaced (`?fps=native`, or the desktop), the player's own work is under half the frame and, under vsync, the frames miss the refresh, it adds `GPU-bound` and the inputs it judged by: `mean 29.41 ms a frame, GPU-bound (guess: unpaced, player 2.10 ms, refresh 8.33 ms)`. Then the mean is the GPU's time a frame, on adapters whose timestamps read nothing too. `tools/web-perf/perfline.mjs` reads it as `throughput`, and `tools/web-perf/run.mjs` gains `--rounds N` and two table columns.
**What to do.** Nothing. A script that splits the line on ` | ` sees one more part.
