### Per-entity ASCII outlines no longer drop a line glyph on a cell border (#493)
**What changed.** A line glyph along a per-entity ASCII entity's silhouette (`ascii_style`, or a `style_mask` naming `ascii`) is now drawn in a cell the entity shows. Before, when the silhouette ran along a cell border, the line could go to the cell just outside the entity, whose pixels pass the picture through, and no glyph was drawn there: a `.` or `:` where the barrel's top edge should be `─`, a one-cell gap in an outline. Of two cells that could take a line, the edge thin pass now gives it to the one the style draws, and a line lying wholly in an outside cell moves at most one cell inward. Whole-frame ASCII is unchanged byte for byte. Per-entity props gain a few line glyphs along their outlines; most of them show only with `outline = 0`, since the `outline` ink floor (#288) drew the silhouette in pixels already.
**What to do.** Nothing. Re-bless a golden of a per-entity ASCII scene if its outline cells moved.
