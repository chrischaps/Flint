### An open spline's sweep keeps its width to the end, and `spline_data` has a frame there (#1016)
**What changed.** The last sample of an open spline had a zero `forward`, `right` and `up`, so every `spline_mesh` (and `sweep_v1`) on an open spline narrowed to a point over its last sample spacing, with a degenerate end cap. That sample now takes its direction from just before it: an open sweep keeps its full width and its end cap faces along the path. A script reading `spline_data` at the end of an open spline gets unit `forwards_*`, `rights_*` and `ups_*` instead of zeros. Closed splines are unchanged, and so is every other sample of an open one.
**What to do.** Nothing. Open-spline track pieces (FlintKart's crossroads walls, verges and shortcut) look different at their ends: square instead of a wedge.
