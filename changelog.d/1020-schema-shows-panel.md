### `flint schema <component>` shows its F3 panel, and `--format json` carries it as `panel` (#1020)
**What changed.** A component whose fields declare a [panel](../docs/book/src/guides/debug-panels.md#a-panel-from-a-schema) (#744) now prints one line under its header, `Panel "Dead calm": status phase, calm, next_in_s · trigger trigger_calm "Calm now", end_now`, and its JSON (one component or the whole export) has a `panel` object: `title` and `rows`, each with `field`, `role`, `label` and the keys that say more than the defaults (`labels`, `press`, `step`, `min`, `max`, `slider`, `presets`, `format`, `scale`, `unset`, `from`, `commit`). A component with no panel prints and exports as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. To see what a controller's F3 panel shows without opening it: `flint schema <component>`.
