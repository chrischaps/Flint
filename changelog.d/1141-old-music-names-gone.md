### `flint validate-suite`, `play-suite`, `render-suite`, `calibrate`, `play-chart`, `replay-chart` and `spike-rumble` are gone (#1141)
**What changed.** The old music command names, kept for one release after #709 moved them under `flint music`, are unknown commands now (`error: unrecognized subcommand`, exit 2). So is `flint render-suite <x>.goldens.toml`, #327's old spelling of `flint goldens`, and `flint spike-rumble`. The commands themselves are unchanged under their new names.
**What to do.** In scripts, CI and docs, write `flint music validate`, `music play`, `music render`, `music calibrate`, `music chart play` and `music chart replay`; `flint goldens <x>.goldens.toml` for a golden suite; and `cargo run -p flint-input-capture --example spike_rumble` (from the engine checkout) for the rumble spike. Arguments and flags stay as they were.
