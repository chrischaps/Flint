### Pencil and watercolor: a paper image (and stroke strip) per entity (#547)
**What changed.** `paper_image` and `stroke_image` on a `pencil_style` entity, and `paper_image` on a `watercolor_style` entity, now draw with that entity's own file even when another entity, or the scene's `[post_process.styles.*]` table, names a different one. Before, a frame drew with one paper and one strip (the table's, else the first entity's), and every other entity naming a file fell back to the procedural paper with a warning. Up to four papers and four strips draw in one frame; a fifth falls back to procedural with one warning naming the entity (`style_drops` in `flint render --report json`). Entities naming the same file share it. A scene that names one image, or none, draws exactly as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A scene that worked round the old limit (one shared paper for every prop) can now give each prop its own.
