### A trace's oldest frames keep their scopes' text: each script's file, each pass's label (#1259)
**What changed.** The scope recorder kept every thread's text details (`script::call`'s file, `encode_part`'s label, a stage's name) in a fixed 64 KB, which on the Overgrown Ruins held the last ~90 frames of them while the records reached back ~600: a trace or a hitch saved after the fact had `script::call` with no file in every older frame. The text ring is now 32 bytes for each record the ring holds and grows with it, so a record's text lasts as long as the record. That costs memory, only on a thread that records text: a default ring is 2.6 MB of records and 2.1 MB of text, the player's 5 s ring 6.6 MB and 5.2 MB, and a ring grown to its most 52 MB and 42 MB. A trace's `ring_bytes_per_thread` now counts the text too (11.8 MB for 5 s, was 6.6 MB). `flint_core::profile::TEXT_BYTES_PER_RECORD` and `Ring::text_capacity()` are new.
**What to do.** Nothing.
