### `--frame-capture` reads the selection mask and ASCII's edge history (#1161)
**What changed.** A frame capture now holds two kinds of image it used to leave out. With `--select`, the `Selection mask` pass writes `selection_mask`, and the `Selection outline` pass lists it under `reads`. A style can now note the textures it keeps itself (`StylePass::held_targets`). ASCII notes its edge cells, slots, thinned lines and outlines, the past edge cells and slots it read (`ascii.edge_cells.past`, `ascii.edge_slots.past`), its tone statistics, variant cells, snap owners and dissolve state. Each one is on the ASCII pass and in the manifest's `targets`. Frames that nobody captures draw exactly what they drew before.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game's Rust style that keeps textures outside `frame.target` can return a `HeldTargets` from `held_targets` and `note` each texture after the pass that writes it.
