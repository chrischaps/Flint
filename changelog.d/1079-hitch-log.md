### `--hitch-log`, `--max-hitches` and `[perf] hitches`: a run leaves a record of its hitches, and can fail on them (#1079)
**What changed.** `flint-player` and `flint play` take `--hitch-log FILE`, a JSON line for every hitch as the run goes (its frame, game and wall time, ms, the median it stood out from, missed refreshes, `player` or `elsewhere`, the worst phase and script, the engine's frame notes, and the eight frames either side). `--max-hitches N` exits non-zero past N hitches after the first two seconds of game time; hitches in those two seconds are counted apart as startup and held only to `--max-hitches-startup N`; `--max-hitches-of player|elsewhere` narrows both. `--exit-after`'s report gains `[perf] hitches:` and `[perf] startup hitches:` lines, and `--perf`'s line and `?perf=1`'s copied line gain a `hitches N in 10s (M in run), worst ...` part, before `cpu`. Either flag records every frame and times every script callback, as `--perf` does.
**What to do.** Nothing. A tool that parses the perf line by position should match parts by their first word, as `tools/web-perf/perfline.mjs` does.
