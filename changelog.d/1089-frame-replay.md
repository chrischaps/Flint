### `flint frame replay`, and the frame debugger steps a frame draw by draw (#1089)
**What changed.** A frame capture can be drawn again from its replay inputs. `flint frame replay <capture>` loads the authored scene, lays the captured world over it by name, writes back the renderer's state and draws the frame, then says whether the final image and the scene pass are byte-identical with the capture's. `--draws N` stops the scene pass after N draws, `--hide <entity>` leaves an entity out of every pass, `--skip <pass>` switches a pass off, `--outline <entity>` marks an entity's pixels; the replay is written as a capture (`--out`), so `show`, `diff` and `open` read it. In `flint frame open`, R replays, a slider (or `[` `]`) steps the scene pass, H hides the selected entity, O outlines it, and the passes can be switched off. `flint render --frame-capture` now writes `replay/` too, as the player's captures do; a missing or changed asset is named. The renderer takes `FrameInputs::draw_controls` (`DrawControls`), off by default: every golden is byte-identical.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game binary that builds `FrameInputs` with a struct literal and no `..Default::default()` adds `draw_controls: None`.
