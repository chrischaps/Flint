### Pencil `transition = "draw"`: a drawing drawn on, stroke by stroke (#879)
**What changed.** The pencil style takes a new option, `transition = "fade" | "draw"`, in `[post_process.styles.pencil]`, on `pencil_style` entities and as `--style pencil:transition=draw`. `fade`, the default, is the old cross-fade: at blend 0.5 the whole drawing is there at half strength. Under `draw`, a blend between 0 and 1 is a front. The outlines grow along their length up to it, the hatching follows a stroke at a time, and the paper's wash comes last. Where the front hasn't reached, nothing of the drawing shows. It works with an entity's `blend`, `tween_style_param`, `blend_source = "tear"` and the table's whole-frame `blend`. At blend 1 it draws the same pixels as `fade`. Rust styles get the hook pencil uses: `StyleDescriptor::draws_blend(fn)`, which takes a blend away from the stage's mix and the framework's cross-fade.
**What to do.** Nothing. To draw a prop in, give it `transition = "draw"` at `blend = 0` and tween its blend to 1 (`examples/pencil_draw_on.scene.toml`).
