### `flint_cli::run_with` and `CliExtension`: a game's own `flint` with commands of its own (#1140)
**What changed.** flint-cli is a library as well as the `flint` binary. A game binary that calls `flint_cli::run_with(extensions)` is the whole `flint` CLI plus the subcommands its `CliExtension`s add, as `PlayerExtension` adds hooks to the player. `flint_cli::extension::subcommands(|cmd: GameCommands| ...)` turns a derived `clap::Subcommand` enum into one, and `.opens_window_when(..)` names the commands that open a window. They parse with the engine's commands in one parser, so `flint --help` lists them and the global `--json` and `--project` reach them, and they answer through `extension::emit`, `extension::document` and `extension::say`. A name the engine already has (`render`, `help`, a hidden command such as `serve`) or another extension's is refused before anything runs. The stock `flint` is `run_with(Vec::new())` and behaves exactly as before.
**What to do.** Nothing. To give a game's tools a command, see the book's Player Extensions guide, "Commands of the Game's Own". A game binary links its own 8 MB stack on Windows, as a player binary does.
