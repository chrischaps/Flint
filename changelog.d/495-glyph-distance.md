### `flint render --reference-text`: ASCII frames scored by their letters (#495)
**What changed.** `flint render --reference ref.png --reference-text ref.txt` takes the reference's glyph grid (the file `--ascii-text` writes). When it's given, a frame the ASCII style drew is scored by mean colour, glyph coverage and glyph layout instead of #183's pixel histogram and layout. This applies to sweeps, `--report` and `--ai-refine`. Without it the score is as before. Every distance in `--report` and in the `--ai-refine` log now has `metric` (`pixels` or `glyphs`) and `terms` (each term's distance and points, which sum to `score`), and keeps `mean_colour`, `histogram` and `layout`. The refine log also has a top-level `metric`, and the terminal prints each score's terms. `--report` together with `--ascii-text` on a still no longer fails with "the ASCII style didn't draw this frame".
**What to do.** Nothing. To refine an ASCII look toward another ASCII render, write the reference with `--ascii-text ref.txt` and pass `--reference-text ref.txt`. A script that reads the report's `distance` keeps working.
