### `FLINT_STYLE_SCISSOR=0` keeps ASCII's edge cells round its entities; `StyleFrame::styled_area` (#930)
**What changed.** With the per-entity style scissor off (`FLINT_STYLE_SCISSOR=0`, `SceneRenderer::set_style_scissor(false)`, the web's `?scissor=0`), per-entity ASCII's edge cell pass is still cut to the cells round its entities, as it always was with the scissor on. Drawn whole, it kept unstyled geometry's lines in the cells past them, and a moving prop's edge history (#477) then differed from the scissored frame's: two outline pixels, the frame after a jump. Now both are the same frame after frame. The default (scissor on) draws exactly as before; every golden is unchanged. A game's Rust style gets `StyleFrame::styled_area(pad)` (`styled_rect` with the scissor on or off) and `StyleFrame::scissor` (the renderer's switch) for state it carries between frames.
**What to do.** Nothing.
