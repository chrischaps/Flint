### ASCII creases mark two pixels, not a band, so shallow silhouettes keep their outline (#484)
**What changed.** The `ascii` style's edge pass rebuilt each surface normal from both neighbours, so a normal beside a depth step belonged to neither surface. Where a step was too shallow to count as a silhouette (the ground just behind a pillar's foot), the crease Sobel marked a band 4 px wide, its cells held more than `edge_max` of edge and were drawn as texture, and the outline broke. Each normal now comes from the neighbour on each axis nearer its own depth, so a crease marks the two pixels astride it. Outlines that went missing near contact with the ground, or along a curved silhouette over a nearby floor, now draw; a few interior crease glyphs on small curved props change. The shared `edges` input (`edges_view`, user styles' `inputs = ["edges"]`) is unchanged.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game with its own ASCII goldens may see a few edge cells move and should re-bless them after a look.
