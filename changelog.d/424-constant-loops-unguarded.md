### User styles: a `for` loop with constant bounds is built as written (#424)
**What changed.** Since #411 Flint has rewritten every loop in a user style's WGSL with an iteration guard (at most 4096 turns a run) and built naga's rewrite of the module. A loop the IR proves ends within that limit is now left alone: a counter that starts at a constant, is compared with a constant (a literal or a module `const`), is stepped by a constant and is written nowhere else, as in `for (var i = 0; i < 3; i++)` over noise octaves or `for (var x = -4; x <= 4; x++)` over kernel taps. A style whose loops are all like that builds its own composed text again, with no rewrite. Every other loop (`loop {}`, a bound read from a parameter or a texture, a counter the body changes) is still guarded. The loops that change ended within the limit anyway, so they draw the same. `custom::Report` has a new `bounded` count.
**What to do.** Nothing.
