### `debug-hud = false` now leaves the panels out of the game's `flint` too, and `flint-widgets` is a kernel crate (#1583)
**What changed.** flint-cli has a `debug-hud` feature of its own (default-on, in `packages`), forwarding to flint-player's. Before, the CLI turned the player's `debug-hud` on in every build and linked `flint-debug-ui` for its windows' widgets, so a game that set `debug-hud = false` still had the package in its `flint`, and `flint build` split into two cargo runs to keep it out of the player. Now both binaries leave it out in one run, and `flint play` from that `flint` has no F3/F4 panels, as `flint-player` doesn't. The editors are unchanged: `flint edit`'s F4 Rendering & Effects panel and F2 graph and `flint frame open` (its frame and profiler tabs) draw with the new kernel crate `flint-widgets` (the widgets, `run_headless_frame`, `RenderDebugPanel`, `StylesEditor`, `paint_style_errors`, `paint_style_drops`). `flint_debug_ui` re-exports all of them at their old paths, `flint_debug_ui::widgets::*` included.
**What to do.** Nothing. A game crate that draws the widgets without the debug HUD can depend on `flint-widgets` instead of `flint-debug-ui`.
