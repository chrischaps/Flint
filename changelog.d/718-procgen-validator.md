### `flint gen` refuses a spec value its generator can't use; `--list-generators`, `--describe` (#718)
**What changed.** A spec's `[params]` are checked against its generator's schema at every depth before anything is generated (dry runs too): a wrong type, a number out of range, a string that isn't a choice or a colour, a missing required key now stops `flint gen` with exit 1, naming the key path (`ops[2].columns`) and what it allows. Ignored keys stay warnings. `flint validate` and `flint check` report the same for every spec a scene's models and textures name (`spec-param`), and the runtime warns once a spec. `flint gen --list-generators` and `--describe <generator>` (`--json`, `--markdown`) print what a spec may say. `flint gen` now accepts the `mock` generator, as the resolver always did. In Rust, `flint_procgen::lint_params` is `validate_params`, and `flint_procgen_ai::check_params`, `IssueKind` and its `ParamIssue` are gone: use `flint_procgen::{validate_params, ParamIssue}`.
**What to do.** Run `flint check` on your project; fix any `spec-param` error it names. Rename `lint_params` calls.
