### `draw_world_text` with `facing` warns when its size reads as points (#1182)
**What changed.** With `facing`, `draw_world_text`'s `size` is the em in world units, and without it points. `size: 15.0` with `facing` asks for a 15 m em, which a camera a few metres away sees as a corner of one glyph or nothing. The player now warns once a label when a plane's size is 4 units or more and longer than the text's distance from the camera (`draw_world_text `> _` ... has `facing`, so its size 15 is the em in world units`). Drawing is unchanged. `flint script api` and the script API reference now say what `size` means in each case, and that faced text reads only from the side `facing` points to.
**What to do.** Nothing, unless the player warns: then give that label's size in metres (about 0.03 for a line of text on a screen, 0.15 to 0.3 for a shop sign), or leave out `facing`.
