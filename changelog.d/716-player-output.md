### The player says what a scene loaded in one line, its real controls in another, and `--log-format json` (#716)
**What changed.** `flint play` and `flint-player` write everything through one log on stderr, under targets `load`, `transition`, `perf`, `report`, `script`, `input`, `music` and `player`. Stdout now carries only `--list-gpus`' answer. Each file loaded is a `debug` line, counted into one summary per scene: `Loaded <scene>: 36 entities, 1 model, 15 scripts ... in 3684 ms; no warnings`. The fixed "WASD / Space / Shift" banner is gone. It is replaced by one `Controls:` line listing the actions the scene uses: those its input config names, those its scripts read, and the controller's walk when the scene has one. New flags: `--log-format text|json` (one object a line) and `--quiet` (warnings, `--perf` and the exit report). Engine load lines in `flint render` moved from stdout to stderr.
**What to do.** A tool that read the player's stdout reads stderr, or takes `--log-format json`. `RUST_LOG=load=debug` lists every file again. A game binary with its own subscriber adds `flint_player::logging::tally_layer()` for the summary's counts, or calls `flint_player::logging::init`.
