### `music_session` plays a suite with no chart; `music_beat()`, `music_bar()`, `music_section()`; the `rhythm` feature (#745)
**What changed.** A `music_session` needs only `manifest` now: its stems play on the suite's buses and scripts read the beat with `music_beat()` (beats from the first, negative in the count-in), `music_bar()` (from 0) and `music_section()` (`-1.0`, `-1`, `""` with no music). A new `events` field schedules an `*.events.toml`'s bus changes. Paths are from the project root. In play the beat follows the audio clock; under `--frame-step`, in `flint render --scripts` and with no device, the game clock, so captures are exact. The component has an engine schema (`flint schema music_session`). With a `chart`, the session is judged as before, now behind flint-player's `rhythm` Cargo feature (on by default): without it the player links neither flint-rhythm nor flint-input-capture and plays the suite unjudged.
**What to do.** Nothing for a scene. A game binary built with `default-features = false` adds `"rhythm"` to keep its charts judged. A game's own `music_session` schema can go: the engine's describes the same fields.
